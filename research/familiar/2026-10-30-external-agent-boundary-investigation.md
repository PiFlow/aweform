## Purpose, scope, and authority

**[FOUNDER DECISION]** Option D is the *provisional working direction*: `PiFlow/aweform` remains the sole organism/science/lifetime authority across egg, stationary and wheeled forms; Familiar is a non-authorizing research programme in this repo; a vendor-neutral assistant and separate physical/firmware workstreams are extractable **only when concrete operational boundaries justify them**. No new repo or approved ADR follows from this direction. The founder's same-battery-powered-core and removable-shell vision is a target, not current hardware permission.

This document investigates the **minimal external causal interaction** required to test whether an Aweform learner can make a prospective prediction about an external event. It does **not** implement the event, channel, intervention, state schema, social output, assistant, user study, physical egg, charger or learned policy. It must not grant any new permission merely by being merged as research.

## What is actually allowed now — verified physics and boundary

**[VERIFIED REPOSITORY FACT]** V0.5's authorised first-slice organism observations under [ADR 0017 §F](../../docs/adr/0017-v0.5-direct-differential-drive-centered-dock-boundary.md) are **exactly eight**: energy, temperature, three station-beacon measurements, charging contact, and two wheel-delta signals; wheel commands use 0.1 s intervals. No accepted external-agent cue, identity or social-output channel exists.

**[VERIFIED REPOSITORY FACT — source-inspected at main `b90dcac`]** [`src/aweform/d045.py`](../../src/aweform/d045.py) declares a **single first-order thermal body**: ambient 23 °C; constant electronics heat 0.15 W; wheel body-heat scale **0 W** despite electrical wheel costs; thermal conductance `G = 0.25 W/K`; capacitance `C = 180 J/K`; no temperature-observation noise; visible value `T/80` (bounded); preferred/protective/hard thresholds 45/60/65 °C. Hence the off-charge steady temperature is `23 + 0.15/0.25 = 23.6 °C`, and passive thermal time constant `τ = C/G = 720 s = 7,200 steps`. The [D-052 controller](../../src/aweform/d052.py) reads energy/contact/beacon but **not** temperature for action selection; [D-053 roaming](../../src/aweform/d053.py) provides an observation-independent proposal. Thermal thresholds remain termination constraints: **trajectory identity is conditional on safe temperatures not triggering termination**.

Charging is a *real competing thermal input*: with stored charging power `P_store` and efficiency 0.90, heat is `P_store(1/0.90 − 1)`. Thus bulk maximum 1.85 W stored gives up to ≈0.2056 W heat; taper stages are smaller and acceptance may limit actual heat. Do not assume an invariant 0.14 W charge heat. The experiment must avoid CHARGE intervals or predeclare charge-state stratification.

**[VERIFIED REPOSITORY FACT]** [ADR 0010 §D–E](../../docs/adr/0010-v0.3-lifetime-plasticity.md) already permits *declared* within-lifetime, lawful learned-state and trace/history mechanisms; it does **not** automatically require an amendment merely to declare a bounded trace. It forbids evaluator-only truth, future events, experiment labels, and undeclared causally relevant state. [ADR 0018](../../docs/adr/0018-three-layer-developmental-architecture-and-innate-viability-floor.md) protects Level-1 innate viability from higher-level learned-state control. [The safety boundary](../../docs/safety-boundary.md) **already permits evaluator-only synthetic sensor-sufficiency diagnostics**; it does not permit introducing them into the organism. No new organism-visible H2 channel or changed heat law has been approved.

**[SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION]** In the *current* V0.5 ecology, the proposed H2 temperature effect would initially be a controlled, noise-free, approximately linear **system-identification/learnability** question, not learned social competence. Its apparent low difficulty is part of the design: a useful apparatus check, not a claim of real-world partner understanding.

## Minimal H2 staged boundary (NOT APPROVED TO EXECUTE)

**Stage 0 — evaluator-only feasibility / learnability audit, recommended *first*.** Under a **future explicitly scoped D-stage/analysis authorization**, reuse existing V0.5 trajectories and source constants. Apply *counterfactual thermal effects by an evaluator-only offline recurrence* and examine an evaluator-side synthetic cue and learner. Do **not** change `D045Env`, organism observation/action, causal plastic weights, Level 1, reward, simulator source, or accepted ADRs. An evaluator-side analytical readout is **not** an Aweform organism learner. Compare unchanged canonical action/mode/energy/docking/termination digests with diagnostics ON/OFF; ensure no threshold crossing. Source-side implementation and experiment execution are **not currently authorized** by drafting this document. Stage 0 can test *lawful-shaped signal learnability and identifiability*, **not** claim that the organism learned a cue or encountered an external partner. Any tooling beyond the existing evaluator-only permission still needs specific founder approval and provenance.

**Stage 1 — future organism-visible H2 shadow experiment, separately reviewed and authorized.** A neutral current cue `c_t` could be made visible to the organism only after a reviewed sensory-boundary change under **ADR 0017 §F**, with causal time/history requirements under unchanged **ADR 0010 §D–E**. The simulated external thermal effect needs explicit approved physics scope under **ADR 0012 / ADR 0017 §H**, including whether it is passive extra conductance or an active heat sink; it is **not** an ADR 0019 wall change. A predictor may then learn from *actual visible consequences* while remaining shadow-only: no influence on actions, external schedule, Level 1 or assistant. An organism-visible external cue is currently **not approved**.

**Candidate effect:** A bounded cooling pulse delayed after a recurring **nonsemantic** neutral cue. The thermal consequence is visible only through the next actual temperature observation (or a lawful history), not via hidden `cooling_on` flags or future outcome labels. In the accepted physics, passive extra conductance `ΔG >= 0` contributes `ΔG(23 °C − T)` and cannot cool below ambient. An active heat extraction term `−q(t)` in watts is a *different physical contract*, not a parameter freely varied inside the accepted source. Choose the narrowest meaningful effect in a subsequent authorized ADR/protocol; numerical illustrations appear in [Investigation B](2026-10-30-h2-shadow-prediction-investigation.md).

**Cue design constraints (future protocol):** no beacon repurposing; cue duration shorter than the minimum cue-to-pulse delay; no cue coincident with pulse onset; cue reliability **strictly below 1** and adequate cue-without-pulse / pulse-without-cue cases; schedule not predictable from evaluator clocks or step position; thermal effect nonessential and, for current V0.5, outside a thermal-threshold regime. A future partner name, `caregiver_A`, value judgement, reward and hidden source label must never be embedded into the organism signal.

**Stage 2 — future active behaviour/partner hypotheses.** Learned output or human-subject effects require *separate* permission, safety/consent review, controls and evidence; H2 shadow success grants none of them.

### Attribution and provenance separation

An eventual evaluator event log (not organism observation) should distinguish: `event_source_kind` = environment process / scripted agent / human / assistant; externally stable `source_id`, `planned_at` and `actual_at` timestamps, action duration/intensity, physics variables changed, cue schedule provenance, and independent RNG streams. These fields **must never be encoded as organism-facing teacher labels**. If an assistant ever causes a world event, record its actual causal identity; do not count that event as a human partner response.

The planned H2 'agent' is **an experimental external process**, not an identified social partner. Partner recognition, reliability, trust, care and attachment are **not** outcomes of H2. External-event observability should be checked *before* any claim that the organism learned an 'agent' rather than time or physical dynamics.

## Confounds and safety obligations for later phases (not first H2)

- **Human-charged portable egg:** manual charging couples energy rise with handling, vibration, presence and sound. Randomized *times* do not by themselves remove handling confounding. Battery interoception may still be studied, but human-specific causal claims require isolation or counterbalancing; a scheduled, person-independent base is cleaner for those studies. A docked nest or charging base is engineered power availability, **not demonstrated autonomous return-to-charge**. Preserve the founder's portable egg and the optional controlled-base comparator; do not construct either here.
- **Modular shell/skin/microphone:** sensor removal is not numerical zero; an explicit availability mask would itself be a new organism-visible channel requiring reviewed authority. Replacing a module can change gain, delay, noise and consequence statistics; log intervention in evaluator provenance. The one organism may retain continuous identity only through a separately justified state/interface migration. See [ADR 0010 §B–C](../../docs/adr/0010-v0.3-lifetime-plasticity.md); no implicit cross-stage inherited weights.
- **Continuity / restore:** exact deterministic checkpoint-resume, rollback that loses intervening experience, and two concurrent hardware instances are different causal situations. Neither a hash chain nor a lease by itself proves unique physical identity. Treat lineage/fork semantics as a **third future document**, not authorisation to implement persistence now.
- **Human safety/privacy:** no unconsented recording, collection of partner identifiers/biometrics or reliance on a public Git repo for human-subject audio/events. Any human-facing signal or physical device requires its own consent, privacy, child-safety, electrical/thermal and device-control review. Do not anthropomorphise failure as distress or battery dependence as affection.

## Decisions, falsifiers, and stop points

1. **Stage-0 evaluator-only observability gate:** Does a lawful-shaped cue/history carry prospective information beyond existing energy/temperature/action and time-only models on original trajectories? A positive evaluator result is an apparatus feasibility finding **only**, not organism learning. If not, stop rather than adding partner labels.
2. **Causal isolation gate:** Are all model inputs and state writes traceable to lawful past/current observations or actions, with evaluator labels excluded? If not, stop before any run.
3. **Level-1/trajectory gate:** In current V0.5, offdock temperature ≈23.6 °C is far below the 45 °C preferred limit; no controller temperature branch. Preserve identical action/mode/energy/contact/termination digests under bounded cue/pulse comparisons, and stop if safety thresholds are reached. The nonessential conclusion must be **reproved** if future ecology couples temperature to behaviour or survival.
4. **Authority gate:** Is there a separately authorized Stage-0 diagnostic task **before any run**, and later a reviewed Stage-1 cue/physics permission before any organism-visible H2 implementation? If not, stop. Evaluator-only permission in policy is not a blanket instruction to execute.
5. **Progression gate:** H2 held-out success is cue-to-consequence prediction only. Partner identity H3, adaptive reliability, social action, assistant writes and human/physical embodiment require new questions and permission.
