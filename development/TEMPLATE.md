# D-NNN — Short descriptive title

- **id:** D-NNN
- **date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **exact_sha:** `<commit executed>`
- **development_seeds:** `<seed(s) or range; must exclude every formal reservation>`
- **disposition:** `ABANDONED | CONTINUING | PROMOTED→EXP-NNN`

## Question

What single developmental question was this run intended to answer?

## World

Minimal description of the environment/dynamics relevant to the question.

## Organism / mechanism

What controller, circuit, learner, or structural change was actually used? Distinguish programmed structure from plastic state.

## Observed

Descriptive observations only. Include useful null/negative outcomes. Do not phrase development-seed observations as confirmatory evidence.

## Surprised by

What result, failure, trajectory, or implementation fact changed the next decision?

## Provisional reading

Current interpretation, including important alternative explanations and uncertainty.

## Next

What is the cheapest informative next step, if any?

---

Keep the record proportionate. Roughly 60 lines is often enough, but there is no hard line limit or universal graduation checklist.
