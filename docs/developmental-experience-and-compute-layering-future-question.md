# Aweform — Future Research Hypotheses: Developmental Experience Cycling and Hierarchical Compute

**Status:** non-authorizing future research note  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-23  
**Repository context:** originally recorded against PiFlow/aweform main at ec2629a917a3a4327652ab9e063f7ea0b84d4bf5; rebased after ADR 0017, PR #159, and the ADR-aligned wheel-legged future-direction merge.  
**Purpose:** preserve two long-range implementation hypotheses about how a future physical Aweform could continue learning from both simulation and lived physical experience, and how learned competence might be distributed across offboard compute, onboard higher-level compute, and deterministic low-level control without pre-engineering a hierarchical intelligence architecture.

These hypotheses are deliberately future-facing. They do **not** authorize networking, physical robot control, offboard learning, cloud compute, a Raspberry Pi, an Arduino-class controller, policy distillation, new sensors, a new D-stage, or any change to the current developmental sequence.

## Research references and motivation

A relevant engineering precedent is:

**Aerobatic maneuvers in insect-scale flapping-wing aerial robots via deep-learned robust tube model predictive control**  
*Science Advances*  
https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aea8716

MIT summary:  
https://news.mit.edu/2025/mit-engineers-design-aerial-microrobot-fly-like-bumblebee-1203

That work shows that behaviour generated by a computationally expensive expert controller can be transferred into a much smaller neural controller suitable for high-rate execution. For Aweform, the important principle is not to import the paper's MPC teacher, trajectory objectives, privileged state, or imitation-learning pipeline. The future question is whether **competence acquired through Aweform's own developmental process** could eventually be consolidated into smaller onboard mechanisms.

This note also extends the future question already recorded in [low-level-autonomy-and-sensor-layering-future-question.md](low-level-autonomy-and-sensor-layering-future-question.md), which asks whether reliable low-level embodied skills can remain useful beneath richer future perception and cognition.

## Future Hypothesis 1 — Continual physical–simulated developmental loop

> **Aweform should eventually be capable of accumulating experience in its physical embodiment, transferring that experience to a more capable developmental compute environment when appropriate, and using real-world experience to refine future models and behaviour. Simulated and physical experience should retain explicit provenance.**

The intended long-range pattern is not a one-time simulation-to-deployment pipeline. It is a potentially continuing developmental cycle:

~~~text
simulation / offboard development
        ↓
candidate learned competence
        ↓
validated deployment to physical Aweform
        ↓
physical sensing / acting / energetic consequences
        ↓
real-world experience retained with provenance
        ↓
offboard replay / learning / model refinement when useful
        ↓
new candidate competence
        ↓
validation before redeployment
~~~

### Why the physical loop matters

A future simulator will necessarily approximate the physical robot. Real hardware may expose effects that are missing or inaccurate in simulation, including:

- actuator asymmetry;
- wheel slip or contact variation;
- real battery discharge and charge behaviour;
- sensing noise and drift;
- changing surfaces and lighting;
- temperature and load effects;
- mechanical wear;
- unmodelled interactions and disturbances.

Physical experience can therefore serve as evidence that corrects or extends the simulated developmental environment rather than merely validating a finished controller.

A future learner might, for example, discover that an action has a different energetic consequence on the physical body than the current simulator predicts. The developmental question would then be whether that measured discrepancy can improve the learned body/world model and future behaviour without giving Aweform a semantic rule such as "this action is bad" or "seek the charger now."

### Experience provenance

Simulated and physical experience should not be silently merged into an undifferentiated dataset.

A future implementation should preserve provenance at least strongly enough to distinguish categories such as:

~~~text
SIMULATED
REAL_PHYSICAL
REPLAYED_REAL
SYNTHETIC_PERTURBATION
~~~

The exact schema is deliberately unspecified.

The scientific reason is important: if a future behaviour is evaluated as "learned through living in the physical world," the project must be able to establish how much of the competence came from physical experience, simulation, externally generated augmentation, or some other source.

### Charging as a possible synchronization opportunity

A future charging period may be a convenient time for experience transfer because the robot may have stable power, reduced locomotion demand, and time for communication or replay.

Conceptually:

~~~text
live / act / sense
        ↓
accumulate experience
        ↓
dock
        ↓
recharge + optional experience synchronization
        ↓
continue
~~~

This is only a possible engineering opportunity. The project should not engineer an artificial "sleep" cycle merely because the biological analogy is attractive.

### Offboard compute must not become hidden life support

A future physical Aweform should not require a continuously available Mac, workstation, network connection, or cloud service merely to continue executing its last validated competence.

If offboard compute is unavailable, the robot should be able to remain within whatever physical safety and viability envelope its onboard validated system supports.

Offboard development may improve future competence; it should not silently turn the physical organism into a remote terminal whose apparent autonomy depends on a permanent external cognitive service.

### Candidate updates should remain reversible

Newer learned behaviour is not automatically better.

A future offboard learning cycle should therefore distinguish:

~~~text
currently validated onboard competence
        ↓
new physical + simulated evidence
        ↓
candidate update
        ↓
regression / replay / safety evaluation
        ↓
deploy only if accepted
        ↓
retain rollback path
~~~

This is an engineering safeguard and an experimental-validity safeguard. A failed update should not erase the ability to compare against the previously validated behaviour.

## Future Hypothesis 2 — Hierarchical compute without hierarchical pre-engineered intelligence

> **Aweform's physical embodiment may eventually contain a deterministic microcontroller layer for body-level control and a more capable onboard computer for learned sensorimotor behaviour, while expensive developmental computation may initially remain offboard. Learned competencies may progressively be consolidated and migrated onto smaller onboard compute where scientifically and energetically appropriate.**

The distinction is between **computational roles**, not between predeclared levels of intelligence.

A possible future arrangement is:

~~~text
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ DEVELOPMENTAL COMPUTE           │
│ workstation / Mac / other       │
│                                 │
│ simulation                      │
│ expensive learning              │
│ replay                          │
│ model refinement                │
│ consolidation / distillation    │
└───────────────┬─────────────────┘
                │
       experience ↑
       candidates ↓
                │
┌───────────────▼─────────────────┐
│ ONBOARD HIGHER-LEVEL COMPUTE    │
│ SBC / edge computer class       │
│                                 │
│ learned perception              │
│ learned sensorimotor behaviour  │
│ memory / prediction if earned   │
│ local experience storage        │
└───────────────┬─────────────────┘
                │
        intent ↓ │ ↑ body state
                │
┌───────────────▼─────────────────┐
│ LOW-LEVEL BODY CONTROLLER       │
│ MCU class                       │
│                                 │
│ motor loops                     │
│ encoder/contact acquisition     │
│ charging interface              │
│ watchdogs                       │
│ deterministic safety responses  │
└───────────────┬─────────────────┘
                │
                ▼
              BODY
~~~

This diagram is illustrative only. It does not select hardware.

"MCU class" could eventually mean an Arduino-compatible board, STM32, ESP32, RP2040/RP2350, Teensy, or another microcontroller. "SBC / edge computer class" could mean a Raspberry Pi, Compute Module, another single-board computer, or a future edge-AI platform.

The project should select hardware only when an actual embodied experiment establishes the required sensing, latency, memory, power, and compute envelope.

### Lower layers may enforce body constraints

A useful future engineering boundary is:

> **Higher computational layers may request actions; lower body-control layers may constrain, interrupt, or refuse actions when immediate physical limits require it.**

Examples could include current limits, motor faults, contact events, thermal limits, charging-state constraints, or watchdog failures.

This should not be confused with giving the low-level controller a hidden high-level objective. A deterministic protective mechanism is part of engineered embodiment/safety unless a later experiment explicitly studies whether that mechanism should itself be learned.

### Compute should follow the developmental need

The project should not decide in advance that Aweform must fit on an Arduino-class device, a 2 GB computer, or any specific board.

The stronger rule is:

> **Use the smallest compute substrate that can faithfully execute the currently earned developmental competence.**

If the active system is tiny, an MCU may be enough. If a later physical embodiment requires high-dimensional perception, richer memory, or a learned predictive model, a more capable onboard computer may be justified.

Hardware constraint should not silently force the scientific question to become easier than the question the project intends to study.

### Competence migration and consolidation

A long-range possibility is that computation may migrate closer to the physical body as competence becomes stable.

For example:

~~~text
early:
offboard compute performs most learning
MCU executes body-level commands

later:
offboard compute performs expensive development
onboard SBC executes learned behaviour
MCU handles deterministic body control

later still, if justified:
onboard SBC performs some continuing learning
MCU executes consolidated low-level routines

future hardware permitting:
most development may become onboard
offboard compute becomes optional research infrastructure
~~~

This is not a required sequence.

The scientifically interesting question is whether a competence that was expensive to acquire can later be executed by a smaller mechanism without losing the behaviour, robustness, sensorimotor grounding, or developmental provenance that made the competence meaningful.

## Consolidation must preserve developmental provenance

A central guard follows from both hypotheses:

> **The behaviour being compressed should, where the scientific claim concerns Aweform's own development, originate from Aweform's own causal experience and learning process rather than from an external expert silently supplying the solution.**

An externally intelligent system may be useful research infrastructure, implementation assistance, evaluator, or comparator. But if an LLM, human-authored planner, optimal controller, or other external expert directly specifies the desired behaviour and the robot merely imitates it, that competence must not be reported as if Aweform discovered it developmentally.

This distinction should remain explicit between:

- knowledge or behaviour supplied by designers/external systems;
- competence acquired from Aweform's own sensorimotor history;
- behaviour generated in simulation;
- behaviour corrected by physical-world evidence;
- competence consolidated or distilled from an already acquired mechanism.

A future consolidation experiment should therefore ask:

> **Can Aweform acquire a behaviour using a computationally expensive developmental process and later consolidate that acquired competence into a much smaller onboard policy or routine while preserving function, robustness, and provenance?**

That is different from asking whether a powerful external expert can train a small robot controller.

## Possible future scientific questions

If these hypotheses ever become active research questions, useful tests may include:

1. **Simulation-to-physical correction:** does adding real physical experience improve prediction of energetic or sensorimotor consequences that simulation gets wrong?
2. **Physical-to-simulation update:** can measured physical discrepancies improve the simulator or learned body model without introducing semantic task labels?
3. **Experience-source ablation:** how does behaviour change when trained with simulated-only, physical-only, or mixed experience under matched conditions?
4. **Consolidation test:** can an expensive learned controller/model be replaced by a smaller student while preserving behaviour on both familiar and perturbed conditions?
5. **Adaptability test:** what adaptability is lost when a slow developmental mechanism is replaced by a compiled routine?
6. **Compute/energy trade-off:** when physical compute cost becomes measurable, does consolidation reduce energetic cost enough to matter for viability?
7. **Layer-failure test:** what happens if the higher-level onboard computer or offboard connection is unavailable? Does the body controller fail safely without pretending to provide missing cognition?
8. **Provenance audit:** can every claimed learned competence be traced to the physical, simulated, externally supplied, or consolidated evidence that produced it?

These are candidate future questions, not a roadmap.

## Guards

- This note does **not** authorize networking or external APIs in the current Aweform.
- It does **not** authorize physical robot control.
- It does **not** authorize a Raspberry Pi, Arduino, STM32, ESP32, or any other hardware.
- It does **not** authorize an LLM as Aweform's cognitive core, teacher, planner, or behavioural authority.
- It does **not** authorize cloud dependence or continuous remote control.
- It does **not** authorize policy distillation, imitation learning, reinforcement learning, a world model, JEPA, MPC, or another learning architecture.
- It does **not** authorize camera vision or new sensors.
- It does **not** change any current organism-visible information boundary.
- It does **not** authorize automatic deployment of newly trained policies to physical hardware.
- It does **not** imply that every low-level skill should eventually become learned rather than engineered.
- It does **not** claim that simulated and physical experience are equivalent.
- It does **not** describe within-lifetime updates as biological evolution or heredity.
- Any future networking, physical-control, sensory, learning, safety, or durable architecture boundary remains subject to the repository's normal ADR and independent-review governance.

## Bottom line

Two future hypotheses are worth preserving without choosing their implementation now.

First, Aweform may eventually develop through a continuing two-way relationship between simulation/offboard learning and lived physical experience, with physical evidence correcting or extending what the simulated system has learned and with explicit provenance separating those sources.

Second, the compute needed to **acquire** a competence need not be identical to the compute needed to **execute** it. Aweform may eventually use a deterministic low-level body controller, a richer onboard computer, and more powerful offboard developmental compute while progressively consolidating earned competence onto smaller onboard substrates when doing so preserves the scientific meaning of the behaviour.

The key principle is:

> **Distribute computation where it is useful, but do not pre-distribute intelligence. Let developmental evidence earn the mechanisms that later become consolidated, migrated, or layered.**

This note authorizes no current implementation and should not alter the present D-development sequence.
