# ADR 0017 — V0.5 Direct Differential-Drive and Centred Under-Body Dock Boundary

**Status:** Proposed while unmerged; accepted only upon Flow-authorized merge
after the ADR-0013 independent-review gate passes on the exact current HEAD,
without any further content change.

**Authorisation state:** This document authorizes no V0.5 runtime
implementation, D-045 execution, learner, calibration curriculum, need
mechanism, or behavioural run while unmerged. After acceptance, it defines a
prospective V0.5 action, embodiment, charging, proprioceptive-information, and
actuator-bookkeeping boundary. Implementation still requires a separate
authorization from the new authoritative `main` SHA.

**Scope:** Documentation-only prospective V0.5 Phase A boundary. This ADR
supplements the accepted V0.3 and V0.4 boundaries and supersedes only the
prospective V0.4 surfaces explicitly listed below. It does not rewrite any
historical source, record, artifact, result, replay, seed reservation, or
evidence contract.

**References:** ADR 0010, ADR 0012, ADR 0013, ADR 0014, ADR 0015, ADR 0016,
the D-042, D-043, and D-044 Development records, development/INDEX.md, and
the V0.5 Phase A authorization issue #156. Open PR #155 is non-authorizing
future-direction context only; it does not establish this boundary.

## Context

D-044 closed the V0.4 docking investigation without changing the organism. On
the accepted D-043 support it descriptively found that coarse station homing
could become very close while terminal dual-contact pose remained fragile;
existing L/F/R signals contained accurate station-relative information on
visited support; shorter fixed forward translations gave mixed local geometry
rather than a robust docking solution; and the forced D-026 delegated action
was locally worse on average than same-state learned and greedy alternatives.

Those are Development observations, not confirmatory claims. They do not
authorize a new action, sensor, controller, learner, reward, or planner. Flow
has selected V0.5 as a developmental reset of the current stage: engineer a
minimal body, physical viability consequences, and legitimate sensorimotor
channels, then let a later separately authorized learning lineage discover
useful organization. This is a boundary choice, not a single-factor causal
experiment; later V0.5 results cannot attribute an outcome to any one of the
bundled action, kinematics, morphology, dock, proprioception, or bookkeeping
changes without a dedicated causal audit.

The V0.5 first slice retains the existing metric scale and arena extent:

- 1 simulator world unit = 1 metre;
- world minimum (0.0, 0.0) and maximum (1.0, 1.0);
- a 1 m × 1 m arena with deterministic body-centre boundary reduction.

The first slice has no causal hull or wheel collision footprint. The declared
body dimensions are morphology and future-hardware compatibility references,
not hidden wall-collision extents. Adding walls, footprint collision, force,
stall, reflection, or other collision physics requires a later reviewed
boundary.

## Decision

### A. One continuous compound wheel action

After acceptance and a fresh implementation authorization, prospective V0.5
work may replace the V0.4 organism-facing action vocabulary
WAIT / TURN_LEFT / TURN_RIGHT / MOVE_FORWARD with exactly one continuous
compound action:

    wheel_motors_lr(desired_delta_left, desired_delta_right)

The values are desired incremental rotations of the left and right wheels in
radians during the next dt = 0.1 s control interval. One compound pair is one
action and one transition. Both wheel commands are independently signed and
reversible. Negative values are part of this same continuous command domain;
they do not create a semantic REVERSE action.

The organism-facing action vocabulary contains no semantic FORWARD, BACKWARD,
REVERSE, TURN, ARC, WAIT, docking primitive, multi-step trajectory primitive,
or internal movement loop. A zero/zero command is only zero requested wheel
rotation. Discrete wheel-pair vectors may be used for evaluator validation or
an explicitly authorized curriculum, but may not silently become semantic
actions.

The durable command envelope is the closed square

    desired_delta_left  ∈ [-0.6457718232379019, +0.6457718232379019]
    desired_delta_right ∈ [-0.6457718232379019, +0.6457718232379019]

per 0.1 s interval. D-045 must declare the deterministic command-to-actual
wheel-response and clamping rule before any substantive probe interpretation.

### B. Founder-selected first-slice morphology and command envelope

Prospective V0.5 uses the following founder-selected engineering design
choices, not claims about Beni hardware:

- longitudinal body length: 0.180 m;
- outside wheel-to-wheel span: 0.215 m;
- wheel-centre track width b = 0.185 m;
- wheel radius r = 0.045 m;
- two independently driven wheels capable of signed bidirectional rotation.

No separate central-hull width is frozen. The 0.180 m and 0.215 m values are
morphology/future-hardware compatibility references; they do not define a
causal collision footprint.

Flow selects a maximum in-place body turn-rate design target of 180 deg/s.
With dt = 0.1 s, r = 0.045 m, and b = 0.185 m, symmetric opposite wheel
commands derive

    abs(delta_phi_max) = (pi rad/s * 0.1 s * b) / (2 * r)
                       = 0.6457718232379019 rad per wheel per 0.1 s

The same derived envelope gives a nominal maximum in-place body turn of
18 deg per 0.1 s (180 deg/s) and, when both wheels saturate equally, a
nominal straight translation of approximately 0.02906 m per 0.1 s, or
0.2906 m/s. These are simulator design choices and derived quantities, not
manufacturer claims. They may change only through an explicit reviewed
boundary update, never through behaviour tuning.

### C. Minimal deterministic differential-drive kinematics

Prospective V0.5 may integrate the actual per-interval wheel rotations through
minimal deterministic differential-drive kinematics:

    d_L     = r * delta_L
    d_R     = r * delta_R
    d_s     = (d_L + d_R) / 2
    d_theta = (d_R - d_L) / b

The exact D-045 implementation must declare whether its numerically equivalent
integration uses the exact differential-drive arc or midpoint treatment and
must apply the deterministic world body-centre boundary-reduction rule. Equal
wheel rotations translate along the current heading; unequal rotations arise
from the wheel equations and are not interpreted as a semantic turn primitive.

The first slice authorizes no wheel slip, backlash, load-dependent response,
collision-body physics, obstacle interaction, suspension or compliance,
voltage-sag motor dynamics, terrain dynamics, or stochastic actuator
imperfection. Saturation and finite encoder quantization are sufficient initial
non-idealities. Body pose, heading, wheel truth, and boundary labels remain
evaluator/environment state.

### D. Centred under-body floor dock

The prospective charging morphology is a centred under-body floor dock. Body
frame contacts are:

    body-plus  = (0.000 m, +0.050 m)
    body-minus = (0.000 m, -0.050 m)

The corresponding station-relative dock contacts are:

    dock-plus  = (0.000 m, +0.050 m)
    dock-minus = (0.000 m, -0.050 m)

Plus and minus correspondence/polarity is fixed; pairs may not be swapped
post hoc. The pair separation is 0.100 m. Charging is true only when both
corresponding post-action pair errors satisfy the inclusive predicate:

    distance(body-plus,  dock-plus)  <= 0.01 m
    and
    distance(body-minus, dock-minus) <= 0.01 m

The exact aligned evaluator pose for station centre (0.50, 0.50) is body
centre (0.50, 0.50) and heading 0. At this pose body centre equals station
centre exactly. The body may pass over the abstract dock plane: no dock wall,
funnel, rail, bump, magnet, mechanical capture, rigid contact, or contact
force is authorized. The contact predicate remains the sole dock geometry
consequence, followed by the retained charger physics.

At exact centre, rotation changes contact alignment while leaving the body
centre at the beacon centre. The derived evaluator-only heading half-window
is approximately ±11.48 deg; it is not an organism-visible signal, target, or
acceptance label.

### E. Retained station-centred beacon

The first slice retains the current deterministic EXP-003/D-020 station-centred
directional beacon exactly:

- isotropic point field centred on the station centre;
- beacon_scale = 0.25 m;
- virtual probe distance 0.10 m;
- left/right probe angles ±pi/4 relative to body heading;
- forward probe along body heading;
- signal(d) = 1 / (1 + (d / 0.25)^2);
- no RNG.

L/F/R remain organism-visible float channels derived only from those
body-relative probe positions and the station-centred field. The beacon does
not expose dock orientation, contact coordinates, true distance, true bearing,
body pose, heading, pair error, or an alignment score. At exact
body-centre/station-centre coincidence, the point-beacon geometry remains
rotationally symmetric; terminal pin alignment must be discovered through
motor, sensory, and contact consequences rather than a hidden orientation cue.

### F. Exactly two quantized signed wheel-delta channels

V0.5 adds exactly two organism-visible proprioceptive channels:

    wheel_delta_left
    wheel_delta_right

They report what each wheel actually rotated during the completed previous
actuation interval, not commanded motion, absolute wheel position, body
displacement, heading, or odometry. Each is signed, expressed in radians at
the organism interface, and finite-resolution/encoder-quantized. Continuous
pre-quantization wheel rotation remains evaluator-only.

The encoder resolution is intentionally deferred because no hardware has been
selected. D-045 must declare and provenance-label the resolution and
quantization rule before substantive interpretation. It must not pretend a
hardware resolution is known. The value may later move from ENGINEERING
ESTIMATE to MANUFACTURER REFERENCE or MEASURED only through an explicit
provenance-preserving update. If the frozen resolution makes the authorized
geometry structurally unresolvable, that is a design blocker requiring renewed
authorization, not permission to tune the resolution against docking.

No organism-visible channel is added for wheel absolute angle, true RPM,
x/y displacement, odometry, body heading, slip, motor current/torque, actuator
saturation, an encoder calibration constant, evaluator pose reconstruction,
or any evaluator label. The organism may retain its own causally available
command history under ADR 0010, but the environment must not echo evaluator
interpretations of that history as observations.

The complete first-slice observation has exactly eight channels, in this
declared grouping and order:

**INTEROCEPTION**

1. normalized own electrical energy;
2. normalized own body temperature.

**EXTEROCEPTION**

3. station-centred beacon left;
4. station-centred beacon forward;
5. station-centred beacon right;
6. binary physical dual-contact charging signal.

**PROPRIOCEPTION**

7. quantized signed left wheel rotation delta;
8. quantized signed right wheel rotation delta.

The channel order is a boundary declaration, not runtime implementation in
this PR. True pose, heading, station distance/bearing, dock orientation,
pair errors, continuous encoder truth, evaluator labels, and experiment
metadata remain evaluator-only.

### G. Deterministic first actuator response

The first V0.5 causal embodiment slice is deterministic apart from any
already-declared environment/RNG processes that remain in force:

1. clamp each requested wheel delta to the declared envelope;
2. produce actual wheel deltas through a declared deterministic response;
3. integrate the deterministic differential-drive kinematics and physical
   contact predicate;
4. quantize only the organism-visible encoder sensation.

There is no seeded multiplicative actuator noise or other stochastic actuator
imperfection in the first slice. The distinction between commanded and
experienced wheel motion must remain available for any later separately earned
actuator mismatch, slip, or load experiment.

### H. Continuous wheel-command-dependent energy and heat bookkeeping

The prospective V0.5 actuator bookkeeping may replace V0.4 turn/move
action-class power with a continuous function of the executed/declared wheel
command or actual wheel response, as frozen by D-045. It must preserve:

- joule battery accounting;
- electronics/background electrical load;
- charger acceptance, taper, and termination semantics unless separately
  amended;
- distinct electrical-input and body-heat paths;
- the ADR-0014 thermal operating and shutdown thresholds;
- energy depletion as a causal viability consequence;
- temperature as an engineered physical viability variable.

The exact first-slice wheel electrical-power and body-heat forms and
coefficients are deliberately not invented as hardware facts in this ADR.
D-045 must declare the smallest symmetric, sign-agnostic, provenance-labelled
model in a pre-behaviour freeze before any substantive behavioural or viability
interpretation and must not tune it against docking success or behaviour.

No docking reward, beacon utility, contact bonus, distance objective, heading
objective, SEEK reward, reward shaping, or hidden controller objective is
authorized. Electrical input and body heat remain distinct as in ADR 0012;
equating them requires an explicit, provenance-labelled sensitivity assumption
in the later authorization and record.

### I. V0.4 control and learning scaffold retirement

The V0.4 behavioural control/learning scaffold stack is not ported by default
to the V0.5 organism, including the mechanisms and semantics embodied by:

- D-011 fixed reacquisition behaviour;
- D-014 departure/controller conditions;
- D-021 controller/mode boundary;
- D-025 stochastic de-trap machinery;
- D-026 one-third delegation;
- D-027 discrete action-conditioned learner;
- D-030 learned discrete steering;
- V0.4 CHARGE / DEPART / AWAY / SEEK organism-side mode semantics and
  descendant controller logic.

In particular, D-026, D-027, and D-030 are retired from the prospective V0.5
organism. They are not silently translated or carried forward merely because
their historical code remains in the repository. A legacy controller may be
translated into wheel-command test vectors only in explicitly labelled
evaluator/comparator work; such an adapter is not V0.5 organism cognition.

This retirement is not a claim that those mechanisms failed. They remain
valid historical mechanisms and results under their original V0.4 boundaries.
V0.5 starts a fresh sensorimotor-learning lineage because both the action
ontology and the observation boundary change.

### J. One-time non-semantic calibration possibility

This ADR authorizes only the future possibility of one externally selected,
non-semantic sensorimotor calibration curriculum once near the beginning of
each new causal lifetime. It does not implement or specify that curriculum.
Any later curriculum must be separately authorized, and its exact command
sequence, amplitudes, repetitions, start geometry, and data-freeze protocol
are intentionally absent from this ADR.

The boundary for that future possibility is:

- it occurs at most once near lifetime beginning and is never automatically
  repeated after recharge, rest, low energy, uncertainty, or another event;
- both wheels may be exercised through bounded symmetric/asymmetric signed
  command pairs;
- commands are described mechanically and never labelled to Aweform as
  FORWARD, BACKWARD, TURN, or ARC;
- the externally selected physical actuation must be recorded honestly as
  externally selected, while the executed command and actual quantized wheel
  consequences remain legitimate organism experience;
- if Aweform infers from its own command history and experienced encoder
  consequences that an external calibration intervention occurred, that
  inference is legitimate organism-visible physics and is not information
  leakage;
- no externally_selected, teacher, lesson-step, semantic-action, or success
  label is added to the organism-visible interface;
- if initial charging observations are included, that intervention must be
  explicit and identical across relevant comparators;
- no repeat body lesson is programmed later in the lifetime. Any later
  calibration or body-check behaviour must be learned or constructed by
  Aweform itself if it emerges.

The at-most-once rule applies to each new causal lifetime, including each
separate lifetime instantiated by a future Development harness. It is never
automatically repeated within that lifetime.

### K. Retained V0.3, V0.4, safety, and evidence boundaries

Continuous-lifetime semantics remain in force: harness/logging boundaries are
not organism events, while an intentional stage reset is an explicit

simulator viability states, not claims of metabolism, subjective experience,
or biological equivalence. The first V0.5 slice retains the accepted V0.4

electronics/background load, one-body thermal equation and state, signed

Reward remains exactly 0.0 on every transition and organism-facing info

## Parameter and provenance rules

The following distinctions are mandatory in the later implementation and
Development records:

- FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE: the selected action form, morphology dimensions,
  contact geometry, tolerance, dt, and maximum turn-rate target where not
  mathematically derived;
- DERIVED: the wheel command envelope, maximum per-interval turn, nominal
  straight speed, pair separation, and other values calculated from declared
  choices;
- ENGINEERING ESTIMATE: any provisional physical response, encoder, power, or
  heat value not measured or sourced as hardware reference;
- RETAINED ACCEPTED BOUNDARY: the world scale/extent, beacon constants,
  battery/charger/thermal semantics, reward/info contract, and ADR-0014
  thresholds carried forward;
- MANUFACTURER REFERENCE / DATASHEET / MEASURED: only when an actual
  provenance source or measurement supports the label;
- UNKNOWN / NEEDS MEASUREMENT: when the repository does not justify a stronger
  claim.

No physical-hardware performance claim may be inferred from these simulator
choices. Once D-045 freezes a first-slice parameterization, it must not tune
that parameterization against docking success, behavioural appearance, or a
desired viability conflict. A later change that alters this durable boundary
or its interpretation requires an explicit reviewed amendment or ADR.

## Consequences

- Future V0.5 work has a minimal continuous signed-wheel action and a
  deterministic differential-drive physical substrate.
- Charging can be reacquired by aligning a centred body with a centred dock;
  the exact aligned pose is body-centre == station-centre at heading 0, with
  fixed corresponding pair tolerance.
- The organism receives exactly eight channels, including only two finite-
  resolution signed wheel-delta proprioceptive channels; pose and geometry
  remain hidden.
- V0.4 semantic locomotion and behavioural scaffolds are preserved for
  historical replay but are not silently ported into the V0.5 organism.
- Wheel-command-dependent energy and heat can remain physically coupled to
  viability without inventing coefficients in the boundary ADR.
- The bundled reset changes action ontology, kinematics, morphology, dock
  geometry, sensory information, and actuator bookkeeping together. A later
  result cannot be attributed to one component without a dedicated causal
  audit.
- A simple or null V0.5 result remains scientifically valid; this ADR does not
  define a success threshold or permit tuning the ecology after observing
  behaviour.

must freeze and provenance-label at minimum:

- wheel-command-dependent body-heat mapping form and coefficients;
- confirmation that no new actuator RNG stream exists;
- retained charger, battery, thermal, and beacon constants;
- exact eight-channel encoding and ranges;

Before interpreting organism performance, D-045 must also perform an
evaluator-only structural feasibility check using frozen parameters: verify
that the declared arena, energy budget, actuator bookkeeping, command
envelope, and dock geometry do not make a nominal physically legal
return-to-charge path impossible by construction. This is a consistency check
only; it must not tune parameters to make learning or docking succeed.
