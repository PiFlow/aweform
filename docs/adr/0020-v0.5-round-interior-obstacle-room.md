# ADR 0020 — V0.5 Phase C — frozen round interior-obstacle room (3 m, rectangular hull)

**Status:** Proposed under S2-A; pending the ADR-0013 formal review gate. This ADR is not yet accepted.

**Authorization state:** S2-A is documentation-only. This ADR authorizes no D-060 implementation, conformance execution, behavioural study, visualizer, or learning. S2-B requires a separate proposal from the exact new `main` and a Sol PASS before work begins.

**Scope:** A prospective V0.5 Phase C substrate/environment boundary for one frozen set of round interior obstacles in the canonical 3 m room. It supplements ADR-0019 and supersedes only ADR-0019's interior-obstacle non-goal, and only for the D-060 substrate. It does not rewrite historical source, records, artifacts, results, replays, seeds, or evidence contracts.

**References:** ADR 0012, ADR 0013, ADR 0015, ADR 0017, ADR 0018, ADR 0019 §§A–G, D-058, D-059, program issue #196 step S2, and PiFlow/aweform issue #211 PROPOSAL_VERSION 3. The proposal base is `94aabf8031da3d35c315c9b2e2322aaa12a6f8e4`.

## Context

ADR-0019 defines the canonical 3 m × 3 m room, centred dock, rectangular hull, endpoint-only room-wall projection, unchanged sensing, and wheel-slip semantics. Its box clamp is closed-form for room walls; it does not define interior-obstacle geometry or contact. D-059 settled the S1 Level-1 disposition. Neither that result nor ADR-0019 establishes how the unchanged floor behaves around obstacles or whether existing lawful signals reveal contact or loss of progress.

S2 asks for the smallest deterministic substrate boundary that introduces a frozen mixed set of interior obstacles while preserving the ADR-0019 hull, room, dock, command envelope, sensing, and energy/thermal contract. Any subsequent behavioural study and implementation require their own authorization. Obstacle handling remains above the Level-1 viability floor under ADR-0018.

## Decision

### A. Sensing

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow; unchanged boundary):** The beacon model remains unchanged from ADR-0019 §A. Obstacles do not occlude, attenuate, or reflect the beacon; a probe point inside obstacle material samples the field normally. This is an idealization that keeps S2 from changing sensing.

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow; unchanged boundary):** No bump, contact, range, slip, force, or current sensor is added. The 8-channel float32 observation, `info == {}`, and reward `0.0` remain unchanged. Obstacle occlusion or any new sensor is outside this decision.

### B. Room and obstacle geometry

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow):** The canonical S1 room remains `[0,3] × [0,3]`, the dock remains centred at `(1.5, 1.5)`, and the ADR-0019 rectangular hull remains `A = 0.090 m`, `C = 0.1075 m`. The S2 obstacle room exists only at `L = 3.0`; the obstacle-free paired control is D-058 at 3 m. There is no obstacle variant of the 1 m ADR-0019 attribution arm.

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow, 2026-10-04/05):** Each obstacle is `O = K ⊕ disc(r)`, the set of points within radius `r` of its centre-line `K`. Obstacles are fixed, rigid, immovable, non-heating, and have no compliance, restitution, friction, or dock interaction.

- **FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow):** Arcs have centre-line radius `r₀ = 0.40 m` and `r = 0.025 m` (5 cm thickness), with rounded capsule-style ends.
- **FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow):** Straight segments have centre-line length `0.65 m` and `r = 0.025 m`; their overall length including rounded ends is `0.70 m`.
- **FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow):** Posts are fixed point centre-lines with `r = 0.05 m` (10 cm diameter), not rolling bodies.

Angles are in degrees, anticlockwise from `+x`; an arc sweep runs anticlockwise from its start angle to its end angle.

| ID | Primitive | Frozen geometry | Opening / orientation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 120° arc | centre `(1.50, 2.00)`, `r₀ = 0.40`, `30° → 150°` | toward the dock (south) |
| A2 | 90° arc | centre `(2.75, 1.50)`, `r₀ = 0.40`, `135° → 225°` | toward the east wall |
| A3 | 90° arc | centre `(0.40, 2.60)`, `r₀ = 0.40`, `270° → 360°` | toward the north-west corner |
| S1 | segment | `(1.175, 0.55) → (1.825, 0.55)`, centre-line `0.65 m` | horizontal |
| S2 | segment | `(0.55, 1.10) → (0.55, 1.75)`, centre-line `0.65 m` | vertical |
| P1 | post | centre `(2.35, 0.60)`, `r = 0.05` | fixed |
| P2 | post | centre `(2.45, 2.45)`, `r = 0.05` | fixed |

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow):** The minimum surface-to-surface clearances are dock-to-obstacle `0.756 m` (A1; rule ≥ about `0.6 m`), obstacle-to-room-wall `0.375 m` (A3; rule ≥ about `0.35 m`), and obstacle-to-obstacle `0.403 m` (A1–A3; rule ≥ about `0.35 m`). The default pose `(0.75, 0.75)`, heading `0`, is legal and is `0.378 m` from the nearest obstacle surface (S2).

**DERIVED:** The arc openings measured between inner end-cap surfaces are `0.643 m` for the 120° arc and `0.516 m` for each 90° arc. Both exceed the hull diagonal `0.2804 m`, so the body can enter each pocket at any heading. The inner surface radius is `0.375 m`, greater than the hull circumradius `0.1402 m`, so the body can rotate fully inside a pocket.

### C. Single-constraint property and contact law

**DERIVED — single-constraint property:** Touching two obstacles at once, or an obstacle and a room wall, requires a gap no greater than the hull diagonal `2√(A² + C²) = 0.2804 m`. The frozen minimum gap is `0.375 m`, exceeding that threshold by `0.095 m`. Therefore at most one obstacle or the room can be in contact at a step endpoint; resolving that constraint cannot create contact with another because the resolved hull touches the first and cannot span the gap. The founder clearance rule of about `0.35 m` is stricter than the necessary hull-diagonal threshold; the intermediate-penetration bound `B` does not enter this derivation.

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow; unchanged ADR-0019/0017 semantics):** Each 0.1 s step clamps each wheel delta to the ADR-0017 envelope and computes `(p_full, θ_full) = integrate_differential_drive(...)` without boundary scaling. The executed heading is always `θ_exec = θ_full`, unwrapped; rotation is never blocked at a room wall or obstacle.

**ENGINEERING CHOICE:** At `(p_full, θ_full)`, classify room-box violation and each obstacle gap `g_i = dist(hull, K_i) − r_i`; contact at `g_i = 0` is legal. More than one violation raises `RuntimeError`. If there is no violation, set `p_exec = p_full`. If only the room is violated, apply the ADR-0019 box clamp verbatim. If obstacle `i` is violated, use the Euclidean projection onto its free set at `θ_full`:

```text
p_exec = argmin { |p − p_full| : g_i(p, θ_full) ≥ 0 }
```

This is frictionless rigid contact: it removes only the penetrating component and retains tangential motion. The final pose must be asserted legal against the room and all seven obstacles.

#### C.1 Finite candidate construction

**ENGINEERING CHOICE — normative construction:** At fixed heading, translating the rectangle while one hull feature contacts one obstacle feature at clearance `r` constrains the centre to a line or circle:

| Hull feature × obstacle feature | Constraint curve for centre `p` |
|---|---|
| vertex `v_j` × point `k` (post centre, segment end, arc end) | circle, centre `k − v_j`, radius `r` |
| edge `e` (outward normal `m`, support `h`) × point `k` | line `m·p = m·k − h − r` |
| vertex `v_j` × segment line (unit normal `n` through `a`) | lines `n·(p + v_j − a) = ±r` |
| vertex `v_j` × arc circle (centre `c`, radius `r₀`) | circles, centre `c − v_j`, radii `r₀ ± r` |
| edge `e` × arc outer surface | line `m·p = m·c − h − r₀ − r` |

The projection lies on the free-set boundary. With one active contact it is a foot of `p_full` on a curve (the foot on a line, or the near or far ray point on a circle); with two active contacts it is a pairwise curve intersection. Enumerate every foot of `p_full` on every curve and every pairwise line/line, line/circle, or circle/circle intersection. Filter candidates against the complete obstacle gap `g_i ≥ −τ_c`, over every obstacle feature rather than only the feature pair that generated a candidate. Choose the nearest accepted candidate; break ties lexicographically by `(distance, x, y)`. This finite set contains the true projection, and every accepted candidate is legal, so the nearest accepted candidate is the projection. Circle/circle intersections cover multi-point self-contact against one concave arc, including two hull corners contacting a pocket's inner surface.

There is no iteration, bisection, convergence loop, or swept test. Candidate generation uses square roots, `min`/`max`, and a fixed loop over at most a few hundred candidates per contact step.

**ENGINEERING CHOICE — numerical tolerance:** A single absolute tolerance `τ_c = 1e-12 m` is used only for computed boundary-candidate acceptance and the endpoint-legality invariant. It is not used to classify contact before resolution and is not a bisection or convergence threshold. D-060 would report its worst accepted-candidate residual and stop if any accepted candidate needs more than `τ_c`.

**ENGINEERING CHOICE — independent conformance:** A future D-060 conformance run compares this construction against an independent numerical oracle. Any disagreement is a STOP. A prototype does not substitute for that conformance.

#### C.2 Derived no-pass-through result

**DERIVED:** Let `R_h = √(A² + C²) = 0.140201 m` be the hull circumradius and `d_max = 0.029060 m` the maximum centre displacement per step: the chord is at most the arc length `|Δs|`, which is at most the ADR-0017 envelope maximum. “Crossing” means the straight segment from start centre `p₀` to executed centre `p_exec` meets an obstacle centre-line `K`.

**Lemma 1 — legal centres are far from the centre-line.** A legal centre is at least `A + r` from every point of `K` (minus `τ_c`). The hull contains its inscribed disc of radius `A = 0.090 m`. If a point of `K` were closer than `A + r`, a point of that disc would be within `r` of `K`, inside the obstacle.

**Lemma 2 — universal push-out bound, for every centre and per primitive.** For any centre `x` and heading `θ`, there is a point `q` with `|q − x| ≤ R_h + r_i` whose hull is free of obstacle `i` at every heading. Since projection selects the nearest free point, `|p_exec − p_full| ≤ R_h + r_i` always, whatever the start pose. The constants are `R_h + 0.025 = 0.165201 m` for arcs and 5 cm segments, and `R_h + 0.05 = 0.190201 m` for posts.

The hull lies within radius `R_h` of its centre, so `q` is free at every heading whenever `dist(q, K) ≥ R_h + r_i`; equality is legal contact. Write `D = R_h + r_i`. Each case either takes `q = x` when already clear, or moves only the remaining distance:

- **Arc:** centre `c`, centre-line radius `r₀ = 0.40`, `D = 0.165201`. The finite arc `K` is a subset of the circle `|y − c| = r₀`, hence `dist(q, K) ≥ |r₀ − |q − c||`. Put `ρ = |x − c|`.
  - If `ρ ≥ r₀ + D`, take `q = x`; it is radially clear.
  - If `r₀ ≤ ρ < r₀ + D`, take `q` on the ray from `c` through `x` at radius `r₀ + D`. The move is `r₀ + D − ρ ≤ D`.
  - If `r₀ − D < ρ < r₀`, take `q` on that ray at radius `r₀ − D = 0.234799`. This is positive, so the ray is defined because `ρ > 0`. The move is `ρ − (r₀ − D) < D`.
  - If `ρ ≤ r₀ − D`, take `q = x`; it is radially clear.

  These cases cover every `x`, including deep penetration, centres beyond the arc's angular span, and concave-pocket two-point contacts. Non-convexity plays no role.
- **Segment:** centre-line on supporting line `ℓ`, `D = 0.165201`. Since `dist(q, K) ≥ dist(q, ℓ)`, if `dist(x, ℓ) ≥ D`, take `q = x`. Otherwise move `x` perpendicular to `ℓ`, away from it on `x`'s own side (or along the fixed unit normal `n` if `x` lies on `ℓ`), until `dist(q, ℓ) = D`. The move is `D − dist(x, ℓ) ≤ D`.
- **Post:** centre `k`, `D = 0.190201`. If `|x − k| ≥ D`, take `q = x`. Otherwise move `x` radially away from `k`, along fixed direction `+x` if `x = k`, to `|q − k| = D`. The move is at most `D`. The no-crossing theorem below does not apply to posts: a point centre-line cannot be crossed.

**DERIVED — tighter bound for convex primitives:** For a post or segment, push-out from any legal start is at most `B` (not needed for the theorem; a conformance STOP). A separating line exists at the start; every hull point moves at most `B` under ADR-0019 §D, so a translation of at most `B` along that line's normal restores separation.

**Lemma 3 — start-to-end distance in one step for arc or segment contact.**

```text
|p_exec − p₀| ≤ |p_full − p₀| + |p_exec − p_full|
             ≤ d_max + R_h + r
             = 0.029060 + 0.165201
             = 0.194261 m.
```

Every other step kind is bounded by the same declared constants:

- **Post-resolved:** Lemma 2 gives `|p_exec − p_full| ≤ R_h + 0.05`, so `|p_exec − p₀| ≤ d_max + 0.190201 = 0.219261 m`.
- **Room-only:** `p_exec` is the ADR-0019 per-axis box clamp of `p_full` at `θ_full`. The start hull is inside the room and every hull point moves at most `B` under ADR-0019 §D, so the unconstrained hull penetrates each wall by at most `B` and each axis clamp moves the centre by at most `B`. Hence `|p_exec − p₀| ≤ d_max + √2·B ≈ 0.1322 m`.
- **Free:** `p_exec = p_full`, so `|p_exec − p₀| ≤ d_max = 0.029060 m`.

The final pose is asserted legal against every obstacle, so Lemma 1 applies to `p_exec` in every case. The theorem therefore covers every step: free, room-only, and resolved against an arc, segment, or post.

**Theorem — no crossing for arcs and segments:** If the segment from `p₀` to `p_exec` met centre-line `K` at `k`, Lemma 1 gives `|p₀ − p_exec| = |p₀ − k| + |k − p_exec| ≥ 2(A + r) − 2τ_c ≈ 0.230 m`. This contradicts Lemma 3 and the case bounds above because `0.1943`, `0.2193`, `0.1322`, and `0.0291` are all `< 0.230`. Thus no step from any legal start under any envelope command carries the centre across an arc or segment centre-line. The margin is `0.0357 m` for arc- or segment-resolved steps and `0.0107 m` for post-resolved steps. This is a proof; the scratch prototype is not evidence for it. Posts are excluded.

The 5 cm thickness is margin, not the mechanism: with `r = 0`, `d_max + R_h = 0.1693 < 2A = 0.180`, still true but with only `0.011 m` margin; post-resolved steps then need the tighter convex bound, `d_max + B ≈ 0.1020 < 0.180`. Thickness raises the margin to `0.036 m` and adds physical plausibility. The proof, not thickness exceeding `B`, excludes pass-through; `B = 0.0729 m` exceeds 5 cm.

**CHARACTERIZED — intermediate penetration and claims boundary:** The guarantee is endpoint-only: endpoint legality plus no crossing between consecutive endpoints. A hull corner can still poke through a 5 cm wall mid-step because `B > 2r`. This remains disclosed and characterized under future conformance check H.6. It is not continuous impenetrability or hardware-valid contact.

### D. Wheels, encoders, energy, and thermal

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow; unchanged ADR-0019 contract):** Wheels execute the clamped command, encoders report it, and blocked normal motion is wheel–floor slip. Energy is computed from executed wheel deltas, which are the clamped command; pushing into an obstacle costs full actuator power. There is no stall, force, friction, or heating model. Energy and thermal parameters are unchanged.

**DERIVED consequence:** The organism receives no proprioceptive or energetic signal of obstacle contact: encoders and energy drain are identical whether the body moves or slips. D-055's stall predicate is structurally dormant at obstacles, as at walls. The only lawful channels through which contact or loss of progress could become detectable are beacon L/F/R channels failing to change as encoder-reported wheel motion implies and their downstream effects on docking and energy over time. Whether that contingency is informative belongs to a later study; S2 adds no signal.

### E. Reset contract

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE (Flow; unchanged ADR-0019 §B.1):** `D060Env.reset(*, seed=None, options=None)` retains the same six option keys, unknown keys raise, the station must equal `(1.5, 1.5)`, and battery, temperature, and latch retain D-045 semantics.

**ENGINEERING CHOICE:** A start pose whose hull penetrates any obstacle (`g_i < 0`) raises `ValueError` and is never silently projected; contact at `g_i = 0` is legal. `D060PhysicalConfig` fixes `room_side_m = 3.0` and the frozen layout, with no option to move, add, remove, or disable obstacles. The default pose `(0.75, 0.75)`, heading `0`, is legal. The obstacle-free control is `D058Env` at 3 m.

### F. Evaluator-only contact telemetry

**ENGINEERING CHOICE:** `D060Env` exposes no attribute named `last_contact`. The following three read-only records are set on every step, reset to `None` by `reset()`, and never appear in observation or `info`:

1. **`last_room_stage: D058ContactTelemetry`**, reusing the existing D-058 type, fields, and formulas, explicitly scoped to the room stage. It is computed from `(p_full, θ_full)` by the D-058 room-clamp formulas. Its `executed_endpoint` is room-stage output `p_room`; removed normal displacement and slip magnitude are the room-stage quantities `|p_full − p_room|`. If the unconstrained endpoint penetrates no obstacle, final position is `p_room`, and this record is field-for-field equal to `D058Env.last_contact` for the same pre-step state and action. On obstacle contact the room is not violated: all `pushing_*` flags are false, both displacements are `0.0`, and `executed_endpoint == unconstrained_endpoint == p_full`.
2. **`last_obstacle_stage: D060ObstacleContact | None`**, `None` when no obstacle is violated at `p_full`; otherwise the record contains obstacle ID and primitive type (`arc` / `segment` / `post`), stage input `p_room` (equal to `p_full` here) and output `p_exec`, push-out vector `p_exec − p_room` and magnitude, signed unconstrained gap `g_i(p_full) < 0` (negative on penetration; the positive penetration depth is `−g_i(p_full)`), active contact feature pair(s) as hull vertex/edge index × obstacle feature (centre-line, inner or outer surface, end cap), active-contact count (1 or 2), and accepted-candidate residual.
3. **`last_step_contact: D060StepContact`**, the unified final record: `unconstrained_endpoint = p_full`, final `executed_endpoint = p_exec`, `resolved_by ∈ {"none", "room", "obstacle"}`, and total removed displacement `p_exec − p_full` and its magnitude (total slip, wheel-implied versus executed displacement).

**DERIVED — reconstruction identity:**

```text
wall displacement     = last_room_stage.executed_endpoint − last_room_stage.unconstrained_endpoint
obstacle displacement = last_step_contact.executed_endpoint − last_room_stage.executed_endpoint
total displacement    = wall displacement + obstacle displacement
                      = last_step_contact.executed_endpoint − last_step_contact.unconstrained_endpoint
```

At most one stage term is nonzero. The records permit later evaluator attribution such as contact episodes, pocket dwell, and loss of progress. They do not grant organism access.

The unchanged `aweform.d052.D052Controller` runs on `D060Env` via new runners. D-055 may be included only if that separate study proposal specifies it. No protected runner is edited.

Any predictor fitted there remains evaluator-only and shadow; nothing feeds back to the organism.

**DERIVED expectation:** Pockets opening away from the dock may trap unchanged pursuit. This is an intended environment property for developmental study, not a substrate defect to engineer away. No avoidance, wall-following, controller change, sensor, or learning may be added within S2 or that study. Developmental obstacle learning and any new architecture boundary require a later proposal and review.

### H. D-060 conformance requirements (future, separately authorized S2-B)

**ENGINEERING CHOICE — conformance contract:** These are evaluator-only probes, with no Level-1 or D-055 evaluation and no seeded lifetimes. STOP on any failure.

1. **Protected byte-identity:** `d045`, `d049`, `d050`, `d052`–`d059`, `vis_d059`, and `development_visualizer` are byte-identical to the authorized base. All committed D-045…D-059 and VIS-D059 records and artifacts are unchanged.
2. **Obstacle-free identity:** For every step whose unconstrained endpoint penetrates no obstacle, including room-wall contact, D060Env exactly matches `D058Env(L=3.0)` given identical explicit reset options in pose, observation, energy, thermal state, transition telemetry, `last_room_stage` (field-for-field equal to `D058Env.last_contact`), `last_step_contact.executed_endpoint` (equal to the oracle endpoint), and `last_obstacle_stage is None`. Also assert that D060Env has no `last_contact` attribute and the §F reconstruction identity holds each step.
3. **Layout invariants:** Reproduce the frozen table exactly. Recompute surface gaps, room-wall gaps, and dock clearance by closed form and match §B. Verify pairwise and room-wall minimum gaps exceed `2√(A² + C²)`. Verify the default pose is legal.
4. **Per-primitive contact conformance:** Test each primitive separately: arc convex side, arc concave side (including two-corner pocket contacts), arc end caps, segment faces, segment end caps, and posts. For each case verify endpoint legality `g ≥ −τ_c`, exact yaw, clamped-command wheel deltas/encoders/energy, projection idempotence, single-constraint assertion, and the no-crossing theorem. Push-out must not exceed the universal per-primitive bound `R_h + r_i` (`0.1652 m` for arcs and segments; `0.1902 m` for posts); STOP if exceeded. For legal starts, post and segment push-out must also be at most `B`; STOP if exceeded. Report arc push-out against `B` descriptively, not as a STOP. Compare with an independent test-only numerical oracle (for example, a dense ray scan): the law must never be farther than the oracle's best free point by more than `τ_c` and must lie within the oracle's declared discretization bound. Any disagreement is a STOP, never a waiver.
5. **Reset:** Check §E, reject obstacle-penetrating starts for every primitive, verify default legality, and retain ADR-0019 §B.1 checks.
6. **Intermediate penetration:** Characterize exact arc samples `k/64`, `k = 1…63`, per primitive and contact side. Report the maximum and attaining case separately from endpoint legality. STOP if any sample exceeds `B` or cannot be computed.
7. **Determinism:** The conformance artifact is byte-identical between the worker run and a fresh `git archive` regeneration, with no RNG and sorted iteration.
8. **Numerical residual:** Report the worst accepted-candidate residual. STOP if any exceeds `τ_c`.

The separate S2-B authorization proposal freezes the exact coverage set: starts, headings, command classes, and oracle resolution.

## Explicit non-goals and STOP boundaries

This ADR authorizes no programmed obstacle avoidance, wall-following, bug algorithm, planner, map, new observation channel, beacon occlusion, controller or D-055 change, D-055 promotion, ADR-0018 amendment, learning, world model, or plastic state. It authorizes no rolling, movable, or dynamic obstacles; friction, restitution, torque, stall, compliance, or heating model; rotation blocking; swept-path solver; changes to hull, command envelope, dock geometry, sensor, energy, or thermal constants; randomized or alternative layouts; a 1 m obstacle variant; obstacle-count or position sweeps; behavioural runs; visualizer; or historical reruns.

**ENGINEERING CHOICE — future STOP boundaries:** A future S2-B worker must stop and escalate if any protected module or committed artifact must change; the observation contract, sensor, command envelope, energy/thermal constants, or dock must change; swept solving, rotation blocking, iteration, bisection, or force/torque/friction appears necessary; any frozen coordinate, thickness, radius, or clearance would change; more than one constraint is violated at an endpoint; obstacle-free D-058 identity fails; a universal push-out bound (or `B` for legal-start post/segment) is exceeded; crossing occurs; the numerical oracle disagrees; telemetry cannot satisfy §F without changing D-058 field meaning; a residual exceeds `τ_c`; intermediate penetration exceeds `B` or cannot be characterized; or anyone proposes evaluating the Level-1 floor, D-055, or seeded lifetimes on D-060 before separate study authorization.

## Consequences

- The new boundary supplies one frozen mixed obstacle layout and a frictionless rigid endpoint projection while preserving the ADR-0019 room, hull, dock, wheel-slip, sensing, and energy/thermal contract.
- The single-constraint spacing property and arc/segment no-crossing theorem are mathematical derivations over the stated full legal domain; they do not establish continuous impenetrability or hardware contact behaviour.
- The organism gets no contact sensor or direct proprioceptive/energetic contact signal. Whether lawful signals reveal contact or loss of progress remains a later descriptive question.
- The fixed pockets opening away from the dock may trap unchanged pursuit by design. Avoidance or learning is outside this decision.
- The three telemetry records are evaluator-only and retain exact reconstruction of room-stage, obstacle-stage, and total displacement without changing the meaning of D-058 `last_contact`.
- S2-A documentation does not authorize D-060 or the five-seed study. Both require separate authorization as stated above.
- Historical D-series sources and records remain bound to their existing substrates.
