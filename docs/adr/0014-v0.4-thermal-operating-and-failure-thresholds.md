# ADR 0014 — V0.4 Thermal Operating and Failure Threshold Separation

**Status:** Proposed while unmerged; **Accepted upon Flow-authorized merge
after the ADR-0013 independent-review gate passes on the exact current HEAD**,
without any further content change.

**Authorisation state:** This document does not authorize D-020 or any other
V0.4 implementation while unmerged. Once its status condition is satisfied,
this exact reviewed text becomes the durable V0.4 thermal operating,
viability, and own-temperature normalization amendment; no status-only commit
is required.

**Scope:** Narrow V0.4 thermal-boundary clarification. This ADR supplements
and narrowly amends ADR 0012 Section N / thermal viability semantics. It does
not invalidate ADR 0012 as a whole, rewrite historical D-002 through D-019
semantics, alter any historical experimental result, or change ADR 0010's
sensory/plasticity provenance closure. It supplements ADR 0010 and ADR 0012;
it does not replace either one.

**Evidence basis:** ADR 0012 and the evaluator-only development audit in
`development/D-019-minimal-v0.4-physical-embodiment-thermal-budget-audit.md`.
D-019 is a development audit, not a hardware measurement or confirmatory
result. The values in this ADR are simulator design choices, not claims about
a final physical robot.

## Context

ADR 0012 authorizes one upper integrated-body-temperature viability boundary
in an approximate `40–50 °C` candidate range, with approximately `45 °C` as a
provisional centre. That text correctly rejects catastrophic-component and
hardware-certification interpretations, but it does not sufficiently separate
a preferred operating region from staged simulator thermal shutdowns.

The first V0.4 slice therefore needs three explicitly different levels:

```text
preferred first-slice operation
        ↓
45 °C preferred/conservative operating ceiling
        ↓
simulator lifetime remains non-terminal above the preferred operating ceiling
        ↓
60 °C protective thermal shutdown
        ↓
65 °C emergency hard thermal shutdown
```

The distinction is a model boundary, not a claim that the organism currently
has a homeostatic concept of 45 °C. The normalized own-temperature signal is
an interoceptive consequence; it is not an evaluator instruction.

## Decision

### A. Explicit amendment to ADR 0012

For the V0.4 physicalized ecology opened by ADR 0012, the thermal semantics
of ADR 0012 Section N are supplemented and narrowly amended as follows:

```text
preferred_operating_ceiling_c = 45.0
protective_shutdown_c        = 60.0
hard_shutdown_c              = 65.0
```

The `45.0 °C` value replaces ADR 0012's approximate `40–50 °C` candidate
range and provisional `45 °C` centre as the first-slice preferred operating
ceiling. The `60.0 °C` and `65.0 °C` values define mandatory protective and
emergency hard simulator shutdowns. ADR 0012 remains the accepted V0.4
physical embodiment, energy, and thermal boundary in every other respect.

This amendment:

- does not rewrite historical D-002 through D-019 semantics, results,
  records, or artifacts;
- does not alter EXP-003 or any other historical evidence, reward,
  observation, action, or seed contract;
- does not alter ADR 0010's sensory/plasticity provenance closure;
- does not authorize additional sensors, reward, control, learning, physical
  hardware, or model complexity;
- does not authorize D-020 implementation before the separate implementation
  task and accepted boundary are in place.

### B. Preferred operating ceiling: `45.0 °C`

`preferred_operating_ceiling_c = 45.0` is classified as a **DESIGN CHOICE /
CONSERVATIVE PREFERRED OPERATING CEILING**.

It means:

- temperatures below `45.0 °C` are the preferred first-slice operating
  region;
- crossing `45.0 °C` does **not** itself terminate the simulator lifetime;
- above `45.0 °C`, the simulator lifetime remains non-terminal until the
  `60.0 °C` protective shutdown threshold;
- continuation between `45.0 °C` and `60.0 °C` does **not** establish that
  real battery, electronics, materials, or other components are safe
  throughout that region;
- `45.0 °C` remains the conservative preferred operating ceiling;
- crossing `45.0 °C` does not generate reward, punishment, rescue, hidden
  success, evaluator teaching, forced action, or a threshold observation.

D-019 records that its reference battery specifies approximately `0–45 °C`
charging and `−20–60 °C` discharge. The `60.0 °C` protective threshold uses
that reference as provenance for deliberately stopping continued simulator
operation rather than implying an unrestricted hardware-safe region above the
preferred ceiling.

For D-020, this ceiling is evaluator-side diagnostic metadata only.
D-020 is a fixed-action physical bookkeeping probe and does not need a
controller that attempts to obey the ceiling. A later controller or plastic
mechanism may use ordinary organism-visible current own-temperature experience
under the existing information and plasticity boundaries; this ADR does not
pre-authorize that controller or mechanism.

The `45.0 °C` ceiling is not:

- death or immediate termination;
- a reward or punishment boundary;
- a binary warning supplied to the organism;
- evidence of subjective discomfort or any other subjective experience.

No binary `HOT` or `DANGER` flag, threshold observation, evaluator alarm
observation, or equivalent label is authorized.

### C. Protective thermal shutdown: `60.0 °C`

`protective_shutdown_c = 60.0` is classified as a **DESIGN CHOICE /
PROTECTIVE SIMULATOR THERMAL SHUTDOWN**.

If the integrated current-transition one-body temperature reaches or exceeds
`60.0 °C` and remains below the emergency hard-shutdown threshold, the
simulated lifetime **MUST terminate via protective thermal shutdown**. This is
a programmed physical safety consequence, not a reward or punishment, a
binary organism-visible warning, evidence of subjective distress, or a claim
that hardware physically fails at exactly `60.0 °C`.

Use `PROTECTIVE_THERMAL_SHUTDOWN` as evaluator terminology if a reason label
is needed. The numerical threshold and diagnostic label remain evaluator-only.

### D. Emergency hard thermal shutdown: `65.0 °C`

`hard_shutdown_c = 65.0` is classified as a **DESIGN CHOICE / EMERGENCY HARD
SIMULATOR SHUTDOWN**.

If the integrated current-transition one-body temperature reaches or exceeds
`65.0 °C`, the simulated lifetime **MUST terminate immediately under the
emergency hard thermal shutdown classification**. This threshold is a
backstop in case one discrete thermal integration step overshoots `60.0 °C`,
later implementation defects or unusual conditions bypass the ordinary
protective threshold, or a future physicalized safety mechanism requires a
second independent software cutoff.

Use `EMERGENCY_HARD_THERMAL_SHUTDOWN` as evaluator terminology if a reason
label is needed. Do not expose that label or numerical threshold to the
organism.

The `65.0 °C` value is not:

- a measured hardware destruction temperature;
- a certified battery limit;
- a guaranteed MCU, motor, charger, or other component failure point;
- authorization to operate a physical robot at `65.0 °C`.

The shutdown values must not later be changed merely to make
thermal-regulation behaviour more interesting. If future measurements justify
a different threshold, that change requires an explicit reviewed parameter or
boundary update; it must not be introduced by post-hoc tuning.

### E. Threshold evaluation order

After `T_next` has been integrated for a transition, the evaluator applies
the safety order below:

```text
if T_next >= 65.0 °C:
    EMERGENCY_HARD_THERMAL_SHUTDOWN
elif T_next >= 60.0 °C:
    PROTECTIVE_THERMAL_SHUTDOWN
else:
    continue
```

This ensures that a discrete update jumping, for example, from `59 °C`
directly to `66 °C` is classified as an emergency hard shutdown rather than
the ordinary protective shutdown. The `45.0 °C` preferred ceiling has no
termination branch.

### F. Fixed own-temperature normalization envelope

The first V0.4 normalization envelope is:

```text
visible_temperature_min_c = 0.0
visible_temperature_max_c = 80.0
```

This is classified as a **DESIGN CHOICE / FIXED FIRST-SLICE NORMALIZATION
BOUNDS**. Conceptually:

```text
visible_temperature = clamp(
    T_body / 80.0,
    0.0,
    1.0,
)
```

The explanatory values are approximately:

| `T_body` | normalized own-temperature signal |
|---:|---:|
| `23 °C` | `0.2875` |
| `45 °C` | `0.5625` |
| `60 °C` | `0.7500` |
| `65 °C` | `0.8125` |
| `80 °C` | `1.0000` |

These examples explain the fixed encoding; they are not threshold labels
provided to the organism.

The visible signal remains exactly one scalar, derived only from current own
`T_body`, ambient-independent in its instantaneous encoding, fixed against the
declared physical normalization bounds, and available through ordinary
organism-visible observation. The normalized signal is own-temperature
interoception, not an evaluator instruction.

The organism must not directly receive:

- ambient temperature;
- `T_body - T_ambient`;
- absolute degrees Celsius;
- the labels `45 °C`, `60 °C`, or `65 °C`;
- `preferred_operating_ceiling_c`, `protective_shutdown_c`, or
  `hard_shutdown_c`;
- distance to either threshold;
- charger, motor, electronics, or other component temperature;
- heat-source decomposition;
- `HOT`, `DANGER`, protective-shutdown, or hard-shutdown warnings.

### G. Developmental interpretation

The long-term developmental intention is **not** to program Aweform to leave
or cool itself at `45 °C`. Aweform receives its ordinary own-temperature
interoception; continued heating eventually has genuine viability consequences
through protective shutdown at `60.0 °C`; and a later development step may
investigate whether experience or prediction can lead Aweform to act earlier
to maintain temperature near the preferred region.

This ADR does **not** authorize that future controller or learner. It does not
add a cooling action or a cooling rule. It does not pre-program a preference
signal, reward, utility function, or emotional valence for `45 °C`; D-020 will
establish physical bookkeeping and expose own normalized temperature only.

### H. Preserve the existing V0.4 thermal physics and null-result protection

This ADR changes the interpretation and declared values of the thermal
operating ceiling and shutdown thresholds and the fixed own-temperature
normalization envelope. It does not authorize changing the existing ADR 0012
first-slice thermal equation or physical parameters merely to make any
threshold be reached:

```text
P_body_heat = P_electronics_body_heat
            + P_actuator_body_heat(action)
            + P_charging_body_heat(charge_phase, SOC)

P_environment = G * (T_ambient - T_body)

T_next = T_body + dt * (P_body_heat + P_environment) / C_thermal
```

Do not, merely to make Aweform reach `45 °C`, `60 °C`, or `65 °C`:

- increase charger heat;
- increase electronics heat;
- invent actuator-body-heat coupling;
- reduce thermal conductance;
- reduce thermal capacitance;
- change battery size;
- change any declared thermal threshold;
- introduce a new programmed controller.

If realistic first-slice physics never approaches `45 °C`, that is an
informative development result. If it crosses `45 °C` but never reaches
`60 °C`, that is also informative. If it reaches `60 °C` or `65 °C` under some
physical schedule, record that without claiming the schedule is realistic
hardware behaviour unless supported by measurement.

### I. Programmed / organism-visible / evaluator-only separation

#### PROGRAMMED / PHYSICS

- `preferred_operating_ceiling_c = 45.0 °C`;
- `protective_shutdown_c = 60.0 °C`;
- `hard_shutdown_c = 65.0 °C`;
- `visible_temperature_min_c = 0.0 °C` and
  `visible_temperature_max_c = 80.0 °C`;
- one absolute integrated own-body temperature `T_body`;
- the existing V0.4 thermal equation and one-body `C_thermal`/`G` structure
  from ADR 0012;
- the existing V0.4 energy/thermal physical accounting, without a new
  controller or learner.

#### ORGANISM-VISIBLE

- exactly one normalized current own-body temperature signal using the fixed
  `0–80 °C` envelope;
- existing authorized normalized own-energy, beacon, and contact signals;
- the existing authorized action interface.

#### EVALUATOR-ONLY

- absolute `T_body` in °C;
- the numerical values and labels for the `45.0 °C` preferred ceiling,
  `60.0 °C` protective shutdown, and `65.0 °C` emergency hard shutdown;
- ambient temperature;
- heat-source powers and their decomposition;
- conductance and thermal capacitance;
- threshold-crossing diagnostics;
- absolute physical parameters and telemetry.

The thermal shutdown boundaries have causal viability consequences, but their
numerical values and labels remain evaluator-side and are not extra
observations. The organism receives only the one ordinary normalized current
own-temperature scalar, subject to ADR 0010's sensory/plasticity provenance
closure.

## Consequences

- V0.4 now distinguishes a non-terminal preferred/conservative operating
  ceiling at `45.0 °C` from mandatory protective shutdown at `60.0 °C` and
  emergency hard shutdown at `65.0 °C`.
- The organism can represent temperatures above the preferred region and
  approaching shutdown through one fixed normalized own-temperature scalar.
- The first-slice visible temperature encoding cannot directly reveal ambient,
  absolute Celsius, threshold labels, or evaluator danger state.
- A realistic null result remains valid and cannot be converted into a
  historical-style thermal shuttle by parameter tuning.
- The threshold values remain simulator design choices and do not establish
  hardware safety, hardware destruction temperatures, subjective discomfort,
  or consciousness.
- ADR 0010's provenance closure and ADR 0012's remaining V0.4 boundary stay
  in force.
