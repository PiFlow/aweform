# ADR 0019 — V0.5 Phase B — physical room-wall contact boundary (3 m room, rectangular hull)

**Status:** Proposed; accepted only upon merge after First Mate QA + Sol PASS + GLM-5.3-Flash PASS on the same exact HEAD (ADR-0013).

**Authorization state:** This ADR PR is documentation-only (S1-A). It authorizes no runtime implementation, D-058 conformance execution, Level-1 or D-055 evaluation, or re-baseline. After acceptance, S1-B/D-058 still requires a separate proposal based on the exact new `main`, aligned with this merged ADR and freezing the intermediate-penetration coverage set; Sol must pass that proposal before implementation begins.

**Scope:** A prospective V0.5 Phase B substrate/environment boundary. This ADR supplements ADR 0017 and prospectively supersedes only its 1 m × 1 m arena clause and body-centre boundary-reduction clause for the new canonical substrate. It does not rewrite historical source, records, artifacts, results, replays, seeds, or evidence contracts.

**References:** ADR 0012, ADR 0013, ADR 0015, ADR 0016, ADR 0017 §§A–B and G–H (especially ADR 0017 lines 47–55, 94–102, and 261–263), ADR 0018, D-054, D-055, D-056, D-057, and program issue #196 step S1 / proposal #201 PROPOSAL_VERSION 4 and its authorizing Sol review.

## Context

D-045 uses deterministic body-centre boundary reduction, not a wall-contact model. Its common endpoint-bisection scale reduces translation and yaw together. An outward command while pinned can therefore cancel the turn that would free the body, reduce the encoder response, and leave the body centre on the wall while part of the body lies outside the arena. ADR 0017 explicitly described this as a simulator boundary effect and deferred walls and footprint collision to a later reviewed boundary (ADR 0017 lines 47–55).

D-054 localized the RETURN wedge to inset 0.00: 128/768 non-docked cases, with onset drain equal to electronics-only. D-055 found that a stall-turn candidate rescued 128/128 cases. D-056 found 15/20 fresh lifetimes with an Arm-U `WEDGED` RETURN. D-057's evaluator-only centre rules that did not cancel yaw resolved all observed wedges (Part A 128/128; Part B 18/18, with 0 harm), supporting the interpretation that the observed wedge was a boundary-rule artifact. D-057 did not establish a physical contact model: its R1/R2 rules had no hull/footprint and selected no tangential-contact semantics. R1/R2 remain evaluator-only diagnostics and are not adopted here.

Flow's founder design choices, recorded 2026-09-30, are a true rectangular 215 mm-wide (outside wheels included) by 180 mm front-to-back hull, axle centred; a 3 m × 3 m room; a room-centre dock; and a 1 m arm available only for later attribution. These are boundary choices, not hardware measurements. ADR 0017's 0.180 m and 0.215 m morphology values were previously non-causal morphology references (ADR 0017 lines 94–102); this ADR makes them a causal footprint.

The S1 question is: what is the minimal deterministic and physically interpretable wall-contact substrate, within the unchanged V0.5 body, command envelope, sensing, and energy/thermal contract, that replaces the D-045 body-centre boundary effect and provides a 3 m room for a later, separately authorized re-baseline?

## Decision

### A. Sensing at room range

The beacon model remains unchanged: `1 / (1 + (d / 0.25)^2)`, with left/forward/right probes 0.1 m out at ±45°, reported as float32 with no range cutoff. In the 3 m room its signal is approximately 0.0154 at 2.0 m and 0.0139 at 2.1 m. These values are small but nonzero and differentiable; L/F/R differences of order 10⁻³ at about 2 m remain far above float32 resolution.

S1 changes no sensor, scale, or constant. Whether the unchanged `reconstruct_source` inverse (`d049.py`, 1e-6 limits) and D-050 pursuit remain adequate at room range is a re-baseline question; they must not be tuned within S1.

### B. Hull, room, walls, and dock

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE — hull and axle (Flow, 2026-09-30):** The hull is a rigid planar true rectangle matching the approximate Beni dimensions: 0.180 m front-to-back by 0.215 m wide, outside wheel to outside wheel with wheels included. The axle is centred, 0.090 m forward and 0.090 m rearward of the wheel-axle midpoint; the 0.180 m height is irrelevant in-plane. In the body frame (`+X` forward, `+Y` left), the rectangle is centred on the kinematic reference point moved by `integrate_differential_drive`:

- `x ∈ [−A, +A]`, `A = 0.090 m` (`D058_HULL_HALF_LENGTH_METRES`);
- `y ∈ [−C, +C]`, `C = 0.1075 m` (`D058_HULL_HALF_WIDTH_METRES`).

The wheels lie inside this rectangle by definition.

**DERIVED — exact rectangle support and free box:** At heading θ, the world half-extents are

```text
hx(θ) = A·|cos θ| + C·|sin θ|
hy(θ) = A·|sin θ| + C·|cos θ|
```

The entire rectangle is inside the axis-aligned room exactly when its centre is in the heading-dependent free box

```text
[hx(θ), L − hx(θ)] × [hy(θ), L − hy(θ)].
```

This is closed-form and uses no tolerance. Each extent lies in `[0.090, √(A²+C²)]`; `√(A²+C²) = 0.140201… m` (DERIVED), so the free box is never empty for either permitted room size. The rectangular geometry requires no search, bisection, or collision library.

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE — room and allowed sizes:** The canonical room is `[0,L] × [0,L]`, with side `L = 3.0 m` (`D058_ROOM_SIDE_METRES`). The only accepted config values are `3.0` (canonical) and `1.0`; `1.0` is permitted solely as the declared attribution arm in the later re-baseline. No other size is legal without a reviewed boundary update. The four room edges are zero-thickness, rigid, non-heating walls with no sensor, force, compliance, or restitution. This prospectively supersedes ADR 0017's 1 m × 1 m arena clause (ADR 0017 lines 47–49) for the canonical substrate.

**FOUNDER DESIGN CHOICE — dock:** The station centre is `(L/2, L/2)`: `(1.5, 1.5)` in the canonical room and `(0.5, 0.5)` in the 1 m arm. The dual-contact rule, offsets, and tolerance remain unchanged from ADR 0017/D-045. The station centre is legal at every heading. The hull does not collide with the dock; there is no dock collision body, consistent with ADR 0015. The Level-1 controllers do not consult station coordinates: `reconstruct_source` uses beacon channels only. A wall-mounted or corner dock is outside this boundary and would require a later reviewed environment step.

Rejected alternatives: a circular hull with `R = 0.1075 m` under-covers rectangle corners; `R = 0.1402 m` over-covers its sides. The true rectangle is the founder-selected footprint. Rotation-blocking corner contact is rejected as described in §C.

### B.1. Reset and dock contract

`D058Env.reset(*, seed=None, options=None)` accepts exactly D-045's six option keys: `body_position`, `station_center`, `heading`, `battery_j`, `body_temperature_c`, and `charger_termination_latched`. Any other key raises `ValueError`.

- `station_center` omitted means `(L/2,L/2)`; an explicit value must equal `(L/2,L/2)` exactly (float equality), or reset raises `ValueError`. A noncentral dock cannot be constructed in either permitted room.
- `body_position` and `heading` omitted mean `(L/4,L/4)` and `0.0`: `(0.75,0.75)` for 3 m and `(0.25,0.25)` for 1 m. At heading 0, `hx=0.090 m`, `hy=0.1075 m`, so both defaults are legal; the 1 m pose equals D-045's body default. Passed values must produce a hull fully inside the room or raise `ValueError`.
- `battery_j`, `body_temperature_c`, and `charger_termination_latched` retain D-045 semantics and validation.
- `D058PhysicalConfig(room_side_m=3.0)` accepts only `3.0` or `1.0`, otherwise raises `ValueError`. It exposes no `world_min`/`world_max`; bounds derive from `L`. Other physical fields retain frozen D-045 values.
- Organism contract remains the 8-channel float32 observation, `info == {}`, and reward `0.0`. Station centre, pose, room size, hull extents, and contact telemetry are evaluator-only.
- Required reset checks at both sizes: omitted options yield the centred dock and legal default pose; wrong station and unknown keys are rejected; hull-intersecting starts are rejected. Rejection coverage includes each wall and corner, including headings where extents reach `0.1402 m` while the centre is between `0.090 m` and `0.1402 m` from a wall.

### C. Contact kinematics and numerical semantics

Each 0.1 s step follows this boundary:

1. Clamp each requested wheel delta independently to the unchanged ADR 0017 command envelope.
2. Compute `(p_full, θ_full) = integrate_differential_drive(p0, θ0, δL, δR)` using the unchanged exact differential-drive arc. There is no boundary scale.
3. Set executed heading `θ_exec = θ_full` always. Heading remains unwrapped as in D-045; absolute sine and cosine make the extents period-safe.
4. Set the executed position to the Euclidean projection of the unconstrained centre onto the free box at `θ_full`:

   ```text
   p_exec.x = min(max(p_full.x, hx(θ_full)), L − hx(θ_full))
   p_exec.y = min(max(p_full.y, hy(θ_full)), L − hy(θ_full))
   ```

   Projection removes only motion along each active wall normal and retains tangential motion: frictionless rigid walls.
5. Never block rotation at a wall. If turning swings a corner into a wall, projection displaces the centre along the wall normal: the body pivots off the wall and the wheels slip (§D). The idealization is that wheel command is kinematically dominant for yaw and the wall constraint is resolved in translation only. No force, torque, or friction is modelled.

   **DERIVED:** The per-step normal displacement caused by rotation is bounded by `√(A²+C²)·|Δθ_max| = 0.140201 × 0.314159 ≈ 0.0440 m`. Rotation-blocking corner contact is rejected: deciding when a turn stops requires torque/friction reasoning, and blocking the turn at a pinned wall would recreate the yaw-cancelling mechanism identified by D-057.
6. Project x and y independently using their own extents. Both components may be removed at a room corner. This is exact projection onto the box and needs no ordering rule.
7. Resolve contact at the step endpoint only; do not perform a swept-path test. An unconstrained arc may make a mid-step excursion through a wall due to translation or an `hx`/`hy` peak during rotation. Each extent peaks at `atan(C/A) ≈ 50.07°` from an axis. The executed hull is inside the room at every step boundary; intermediate penetration is separately characterized under §D.
8. Use float64 pose math; use `math.cos`/`math.sin`, then `abs`, `min`, and `max`. Do not use bisection or epsilon. A projected coordinate equals its extent bound; the D-045 0.0-versus-1.0 bisection asymmetry is absent.
9. Reject reset poses whose hull is not fully inside the room at the start heading. Never silently project an illegal reset pose. The executable reset contract is specified in §B.1.

### D. Fidelity and claims boundary

The S1 wall law is a **time-discrete, endpoint-constrained, rigid-rectangle, frictionless-tangent kinematic idealization**, a declared first model. It is not a swept-path collision model, not force closure, not a claim of continuous impenetrability, and not hardware-valid wall contact.

Intermediate penetration is expected and disclosed: within a 0.1 s step, a hull corner or translating body may penetrate a wall along the unconstrained arc even though the projected endpoint pose is legal. This limitation must be stated in the D-058 record and in the later re-baseline interpretation contract. No re-baseline result may be phrased as validating continuous contact, hardware wall behaviour, or force/slip realism.

**DERIVED — intermediate-penetration bound:** From a legal start pose, a loose worst-case bound (translation and rotation maxima cannot co-occur) is

```text
B = 2·√(A²+C²)·sin(Δθ_max/2) + d_max
  = 0.043864 + 0.029060
  ≈ 0.07292419 m (about 0.072924 m when truncated to six decimals)
```

The symbolic expression is authoritative. `0.072924 m` is a truncated display only, not a mathematically exact upper bound or a stricter STOP threshold. Here `Δθ_max = π/10` (18°) and `d_max = 0.029060 m` are the ADR 0017 envelope maxima. D-058 must stop and return for review if any sampled penetration exceeds the symbolic `B`, if the endpoint invariant fails, or if the characterization cannot be completed; it may not silently promote the claim to continuous contact or add an unreviewed swept solver.

D-058 must evaluate intermediate poses exactly on the unconstrained arc. Scaling both clamped wheel deltas by `k/64`, `k=1…63`, gives exact intermediate arc poses. It must also evaluate analytic extent-peak headings inside the step's heading interval: `hx` peaks at `θ ≡ ±φ mod π`, `hy` at `θ ≡ ±(π/2 − φ) mod π`, where `φ = atan(C/A)`, each at its arc fraction. The S1-B authorization proposal freezes the coverage set. At minimum it includes, for each wall and corner: flush and near-contact (`+0.01 m`) starts; headings every 15° plus peak headings; ADR 0017 envelope-extreme wheel pairs; and the D-054 onset command `(-0.565, +0.646)`. Report maximum sampled penetration overall and per wall/corner/command class, separately from endpoint legality, and identify the attaining case.

### E. Wheels, encoders, sensing, and energy/thermal bookkeeping

**ENGINEERING ESTIMATE:** Retained actuator/energy/thermal parameter values are those frozen in D-045 under ADR 0017; they are not re-estimated by S1. The physical reading is velocity-controlled drive wheels. With no force or traction model, each wheel executes its clamped command in full. When normal body motion is blocked, the difference between wheel-implied and executed body motion is wheel–floor slip.

Encoders measure wheel-shaft rotation: `wheel_delta_left/right = quantize_wheel_delta(clamped command)` at walls exactly as in free space. The 8-channel float32 observation and channel semantics/quantization are unchanged. There is no bump, contact, slip, force, current, or wall sensor. ADR 0017 §G is preserved (lines 261–263): commanded wheel rotation and experienced body motion can differ at walls, in evaluator telemetry only, leaving any slip/odometry experiment for a later separately earned boundary.

The rejected stall alternative (wheels stop and encoders read zero) requires a motor-torque-versus-traction threshold and a definition of “blocked” for oblique contact, introducing force-model reasoning excluded by ADR 0012, ADR 0015, and ADR 0017.

**Consequence:** under slip semantics the organism has no lawful proprioceptive signal of wall contact. D-055's predicate—both wheel-delta channels exactly zero after a RETURN command at or above the quantum—cannot fire at a wall by construction. Its dormancy in a future re-baseline must be reported as structural, never as evidence that stall recovery is unnecessary for its own sake. The re-baseline question remains whether the unchanged floor fails on S1. A U failure on S1 does not by itself authorize permanent D-055 promotion; that question belongs to the paired re-baseline and any separate mechanism/ADR decision.

The frozen energy model is unchanged: `wheel_effort` from executed wheel deltas; `D045_WHEEL_POWER_SCALE_W = 1.0`; `D045_WHEEL_BODY_HEAT_SCALE_W = 0.0`; electronics 0.15 W; battery 5328 J; unchanged thermal constants and thresholds. Since executed wheel deltas are the clamped command, pushing against a wall costs full actuator power (up to 0.115 J per step at full effort), rather than the electronics-only 0.015 J per step that made the D-045 wedge last about 71k steps. There is no wall heating, stall current, slip-friction dissipation, or thermal-stall model; adding any is a later reviewed boundary.

Declared consequences, not new acceptance claims:

- Under the D-053 fixture, roaming touched walls on about 57–85% of transitions in D-045 lifetimes; under S1 these transitions change trajectory, energy use, and cycle timing.
- **DERIVED:** The farthest legal centre is about 2.0 m from the dock at a room corner, and forward probes reach about 2.1 m. A straight-line return is at most approximately `2.0 m / 0.2906 m s⁻¹ ≈ 6.9 s ≈ 69 steps` at full speed, costing approximately 7.9 J against the 1,065.6 J RETURN reserve.
- D-053…D-056 lifetime numbers are not comparable with S1 and remain bound to D-045.

### F. Evaluator-only contact telemetry

`D058Env` exposes a read-only per-step evaluator record (for example `last_contact`) that is never in the observation or `info`. It contains the unconstrained and executed endpoints; per-wall `pushing_{x_min,x_max,y_min,y_max}` flags for active projection; `hx(θ_full)` and `hy(θ_full)` and removed normal displacement; and wheel-implied versus executed displacement (slip magnitude). The D-045 `boundary_scale` has no S1 analogue and is not emulated.

### H. Re-baseline interface (defined here, not authorized)

The following defines a future interface only; this ADR does not authorize re-baseline runs.

- **API:** `D058Env` keeps the `D045Env` Gymnasium API: `reset(*, seed, options)` returns `(observation, info)` with the same evaluator setup options; `step(action)` returns `(observation float32[8], 0.0, terminated, truncated, {})`, with D-045 termination semantics and the same `D045Observation` type. The only D-045 differences are dynamics and reset legality.
- **Controllers:** the unchanged floor `aweform.d052.D052Controller` (delegating to `aweform.d050.D050SmoothController`) and comparator `aweform.d055.D055StallTurnCandidate` remain pure functions of observation and are imported byte-identically.
- **Runners:** new runners are required because protected existing runners hard-wire D045Env. S1-B provides only an env-factory seam in `d058.py` and edits no protected runner.
- **Support:** D-054 insets 0.00/0.05 and D-050/D-052 0.45 m radii put centres within 0.05–0.065 m of a wall; these poses are illegal under S1, where a centre must remain 0.090–0.1402 m from a wall depending on heading. A re-baseline proposal must define legal starts (for example insets measured from hull contact), fresh seeded D-053-fixture lifetimes, and state that D-054/55/56 cannot be replayed verbatim.
- **Attribution:** the 3 m canonical S1 room versus the 1 m S1 arm, with identical rectangle, wall rule, centred dock, and unchanged U/C controllers, is a paired **room-size** contrast on S1. The 1 m arm alone does not attribute a wall-rule effect. Any wall-rule outcome claim requires a separate-proposal matched 1 m D-045-versus-D-058 comparison with starts legal in both, identical headings, battery, temperature, dock `(0.5,0.5)`, horizons, and unchanged U/C controllers. Historical D-054/55/56 counts are descriptive context only; no unmatched comparison is a single-factor causal estimate.

### I. D-058 structural-feasibility / parameter-freeze requirements (future S1-B)

This evaluator-only D-058 check is analogous to ADR 0017's D-045 parameter freeze. It uses no Level-1 floor or D-055 evaluation and no seeded lifetimes. All checks are requirements on a future, separately authorized S1-B implementation; they are not performed by this documentation-only ADR. STOP on any failure.

1. `d045.py` and every protected module listed in §G are byte-identical to the authorized base.
2. **Free-space identity at both `L=3.0` and `L=1.0`:** from a legal pose whose full endpoint rectangle lies inside at `θ_full`, D058Env produces the same pose, observation, energy, and thermal state as the oracle. The oracle is D045Env using its existing `D045PhysicalConfig` `world_min`/`world_max` seam for `[0,L]`. Supply both environments identical explicit reset options: `station_center=(L/2,L/2)`, body position, heading, battery, body temperature, and charger-termination latch; do not let D-045 fall back to its differing station default. Do not modify `d045.py`.
3. **Projection invariants:** independently test all four executed world-space rectangle corners inside the arena; projection is idempotent; yaw equals unconstrained yaw; wheel deltas and encoder counts equal the clamped command; effort and energy identities hold.
4. **Wall/corner cases:** head-on; oblique with exact tangential retention; corner with both components removed; and pure spin against a wall at several headings, with centre pivoting off along the normal, exact yaw, and displacement ≤ 0.0440 m per step. Include D-054 onset command `(-0.565,+0.646)` at the bottom wall. Its S1 turning/sliding without yaw cancellation is descriptive only.
5. **Reset/dock/envelope:** check §B.1 reset requirements at both room sizes (defaults, wrong-station rejection, unknown-key rejection, hull-intersecting-start rejection); dock reachability at the station centre; and legality of the full ADR 0017 command envelope from every legal pose.
6. **Determinism:** conformance artifact is byte-identical between the worker run and fresh `git archive` regeneration. Use no RNG and no hash-order dependence; sort any set iteration, following D-057.
7. **Intermediate penetration:** characterize the §D exact arc samples and extent-peak headings over the frozen coverage set; report maximum and attaining case separately from the endpoint invariant; STOP if any sample exceeds the symbolic `B` in §D or characterization cannot be completed.

## Explicit non-goals

This ADR does not authorize:

- interior or round obstacles (S2), generic obstacle avoidance, wall-following, or a wall sensor;
- a new observation channel (bump, contact, slip, force, or current);
- force, torque, traction, friction coefficient, compliance, restitution, stall, thermal-stall, or wall-heating models;
- changes to morphology constants, command envelope, dock contact geometry, beacon/sensor constants, power/thermal constants, or battery; the only world changes are the 3 m room (with 1 m attribution arm) and room-centre dock;
- wall-mounted/corner docks, furniture, doors, or non-rectangular rooms;
- Level-1 changes, permanent D-055 promotion, or ADR-0018 amendment (step P, if ever justified);
- learning, a planner, or a world model;
- re-baseline runs or historical reruns;
- adoption of D-057 R1/R2. S1's projection resembles R2's endpoint clamp only in form, now applied to heading-dependent rectangle bounds and justified independently as frictionless rigid-wall contact.

## Consequences

- The D-045 body-centre boundary effect is prospectively replaced for the new substrate by a finite rectangular footprint and an endpoint projection that preserves full yaw and tangential motion.
- The 3 m room is canonical; 1 m is an attribution-only arm. The dock is centred at either room size.
- Wheel-shaft rotation and encoders follow the full clamped command at walls; body motion can differ through evaluator-visible slip, with no organism-visible wall/contact cue.
- The energy/thermal model and observation contract remain unchanged, while wall pushing now consumes full actuator energy.
- Intermediate penetration can occur within a step. The boundary is a declared first kinematic idealization, not continuous-contact or hardware validation.
- D-055 wall-stall detection is structurally dormant under slip semantics. A U failure on S1 alone does not authorize D-055 promotion.
- D-057 R1/R2 remain evaluator-only diagnostics and are not adopted.
- Historical D-045…D-057 results remain tied to D-045; room-size and wall-rule attribution require the distinct controls specified in §H.
