# ADR 0010 — V0.3 Opens: Lifetime Plasticity and Sensory/Plasticity Closure

**Status:** Accepted

**Authorisation:** Flow, 2026-08-27, after independent Sol + Claude Opus 5 architecture convergence. This ADR defines the permission boundary for V0.3 development; it does not freeze a particular learner or experiment protocol.

**Opens:** V0.3. **References, and does not amend or invalidate,** ADR 0009 or earlier historical experiment records.

## Context

ADR 0009 opened V0.2 by permitting bounded one-step observation-history state under a deliberately restrictive shape and registry. That boundary remains the valid historical authorization for V0.2 work performed under it.

The next developmental stage introduces genuine lifetime plasticity. Shape restrictions that were useful for one-step history cannot serve as the permanent learning boundary: learned parameters, arithmetic updates, recurrent state, eligibility, adaptive plasticity, and bounded experience retention may all become legitimate mechanisms.

The durable scientific requirement is instead **provenance**: evaluator truth must not become a hidden teacher.

V0.3 therefore constrains what information may causally write organism state rather than prescribing one learner architecture.

## Decision

### A. V0.3 permits bounded lifetime plasticity

Within a declared organism lifetime, a V0.3 organism may retain bounded persistent plastic/learned state and use it to alter future behaviour.

This ADR does **not** select or pre-authorize:

- a six-gain learner or any fixed parameter count;
- a fixed prediction horizon `k`;
- a constant learning rate or update schedule as a permanent rule;
- linear/additive prediction;
- action-conditioned prediction;
- a reservoir;
- a particular arbitration function;
- thermal constants or distance-dependent cooling;
- reinforcement learning, PPO, deep networks, JEPA-scale models, or LLM cognition.

Specific mechanisms must earn their place through development.

### B. A lifetime is one continuous causal trajectory

Death is final for that lifetime.

Harness horizons, logging windows, visualization windows, storage chunks, or future checkpoint boundaries are measurement/engineering boundaries only. They are not organism rebirths or organism-visible events.

Within one lifetime, such segmentation must not reset or reseed causally relevant state, including:

- learned/plastic state;
- transient controller state;
- circuit/filter/history state;
- policy/explorer phase;
- organism-owned RNG state.

A harness boundary must not expose a segment identifier, artificial fresh-start signal, wall-clock value, or boundary-derived clock to the organism.

If segmented/checkpointed lifetime execution is later implemented, it must preserve the complete causal continuation state. Deterministic implementations should include a segmented-versus-uninterrupted equivalence test.

### C. Developmental-stage resets are explicit lifecycle events

A deliberate developmental stage boundary may introduce new sensors, internal variables, circuits, structural capabilities, or morphology.

Under the current convention, learned/plastic state resets at a developmental-stage boundary. Such a reset is explicitly recorded as a lifecycle/new-lifetime event and must never be disguised as ordinary harness segmentation.

Cross-stage inheritance of learned state is deferred as its own later research question.

V0.3 does not authorize population evolution, heredity, mutation, Darwinian selection, or evolutionary optimization as a shortcut for bootstrapping the first learner.

### D. Sensory/plasticity closure

A causal plastic update may depend only on information that is legitimately available to the organism at the moment of update:

- declared organism-visible sensory channels;
- declared organism-visible interoceptive channels;
- legitimately retained internal state;
- the organism's own causally available past/current actions;
- organism-visible consequences that have actually occurred by update time;
- current learned/plastic state;
- declared structural constants;
- explicitly declared organism-owned RNG state when randomness is part of the mechanism.

A plastic update may not receive:

- evaluator coordinates;
- true distance or hidden geometry;
- coverage or lifespan metrics;
- evaluator success/failure labels;
- experiment-condition labels;
- reserved-seed identity as a learning input;
- hidden environment state;
- future observations or consequences not yet sensed;
- human task reward;
- evaluator telemetry repackaged as a learning target.

### E. Recursive provenance applies to every retained causal object

Retained state is legitimate only if every causal write contributing to it obeyed this same closure rule.

"Plastic/learned state" means every retained object whose value can influence future learning or behaviour, not merely headline weights. Where present this includes, for example:

- learned parameters;
- optimizer or adaptation state;
- eligibility traces;
- recurrent state;
- adaptive learning-rate state;
- plasticity-modulation state;
- consolidation state;
- bounded experience buffers;
- organism-owned RNG state;
- any other retained causal learning object.

Each plastic mechanism must declare, at source/repository level and proportionately to its complexity:

- organism-visible input channels;
- persistent internal-state fields/dimensions;
- plastic parameter/state dimensions;
- update inputs;
- update timing;
- retention/reset semantics;
- organism-owned RNG use where applicable.

The project should use inspectable declarations and focused tests rather than rebuilding a large generic enforcement framework before it is needed.

### F. Plasticity timing and modulation remain mechanism-level decisions

A specific learner may use a fixed horizon, fixed learning rate, or fixed update schedule. Those are experimental simplifications, not universal V0.3 laws.

Future mechanisms may modulate plasticity using closure-valid organism-visible or internal state, including prediction error, energy, temperature, uncertainty, or later bounded consolidation state. The modulation itself must be declared and closure-valid.

Causal availability plus bounded declared retention is the durable rule.

### G. Initialization and matched comparisons

For matched learning comparisons, organisms should normally begin from identical declared architecture and plastic initialization unless initialization is itself the manipulated variable.

Development may explore alternatives, but evidence claims about individuality through experience must isolate history from initial-state differences.

### H. No hidden viability controller in the first simulated learner

V0.3 does not authorize a controller-side hard viability override whose frequent intervention could make plasticity decorative while preserving apparent competence.

Death and viability loss remain genuine simulated consequences.

Future computer-native or physical embodiment safety interlocks are separate embodiment-level safety boundaries and are not prohibited by this rule.

## Future common-probe implication

A later history-divergence evidence design must equalize non-plastic probe state and transfer the **complete declared plastic state**, not merely primary weights. Depending on the learner this may include optimizer/adaptation state, eligibility, consolidation state, plasticity modulation, recurrent learned state, and organism-owned RNG state.

The exact EXP-004 protocol is intentionally not frozen by this ADR.

## Consequences

- ADR 0009 remains historically correct for V0.2.
- V0.3 permits genuine lifetime learning without making the evaluator the teacher.
- Developmental stage changes remain explicit interventions rather than silent carry-over.
- Quiescent consolidation remains a future hypothesis; it is not part of the first learner merely because biological organisms sleep.
- Failure of a simple predictor is not by itself evidence that a larger learner is required; omitted state, partial observability, stochasticity, and model capacity must remain distinguishable explanations.
