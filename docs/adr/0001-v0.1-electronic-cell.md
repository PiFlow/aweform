# ADR 0001 — V0.1 Is the Electronic-Cell Stage

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Aweform's long-term goal is to investigate machine-native artificial life and adaptive cognition. The project could easily become over-designed by beginning with mature capabilities such as vision models, reinforcement learning, language, planning, or social behaviour.

The first credible developmental step is smaller: construct a bounded simulated organism whose continued viability depends on maintaining an internal energetic state through local sensing and action.

"Electronic cell" is a developmental analogy, not a biological claim. V0.1 does not attempt to reproduce cellular biochemistry, metabolism, reproduction, heredity, or Darwinian evolution.

## Decision

V0.1 will implement only the minimum substrate necessary to study homeostasis and interoception.

The intended V0.1 substrate includes:

- finite internal energy;
- explicit energetic cost of continued existence and/or action;
- environmental energy resources;
- limited local sensing;
- internal energy sensing (interoception);
- a small bounded action set;
- finite episodes;
- deterministic/reproducible experiment execution;
- transparent comparator controllers suitable for ablation.

The energy variable is an engineered viability state, not a biological-metabolism claim and not a reward score. It must be causally connected to the organism's ability to continue sensing/acting and to episode viability; otherwise it is only decorative bookkeeping.

V0.1 should use simple configurable energy costs rather than attempting to model real CPU joules or battery chemistry. Physical energy grounding belongs to later embodiment experiments.

The first implementation target is EXP-000: Interoception and Viability.

## Non-goals

V0.1 does not include:

- LLMs or human language;
- neural networks;
- reinforcement learning or PPO;
- JEPA or another learned world model;
- memory, except the single bounded one-step mechanism narrowly authorised by
  [`ADR 0009`](0009-bounded-one-step-beacon-trend-memory.md);
- curiosity or play;
- an awe mechanism;
- social behaviour;
- camera/image perception;
- obstacles or complex physics;
- ROS or physical robotics;
- networking;
- self-modification;
- reproduction;
- evolutionary optimisation;
- open-ended autonomy.

These are not rejected permanently. They are deferred until a later developmental problem justifies them.

## Reward decision

For EXP-000, the environment's reinforcement-learning reward remains `0.0` on every transition.

Scientific evaluation uses direct viability and behavioural measurements rather than an optimisation reward. Changing this requires an explicit later decision.

## Confirmatory-experiment gate

Before any confirmatory EXP-000 run, the project must freeze and record:

- the environment and observation/action contract;
- energy costs, viability thresholds, and termination rules;
- comparator-controller logic and hysteresis thresholds;
- the exact energy-blind ablation method;
- calibration/development seeds and untouched acceptance seeds;
- primary outcomes;
- the directional and effect-size criteria used to interpret a pass, weak result, or null result.

Those choices may be explored during calibration, but they must not be changed after inspecting acceptance-set outcomes without declaring a new experiment version.

## Design principle

The first controller should be deliberately transparent. Programmed homeostatic switching is an experimental condition, not a claim of emergent intelligence.

The primary causal comparison is the intact homeostatic controller versus an otherwise closely matched controller deprived of informative interoception. A random/persistent-exploration condition is a reference baseline for task difficulty, not the sole causal control.

The purpose of V0.1 is to establish a clean substrate on which later developmental mechanisms can be tested, not to demonstrate sophisticated behaviour.

## Consequences

The initial codebase should remain small and easy to inspect. Dependencies and abstractions that exist only for anticipated later stages should not be added.

Success means the experiment is reproducible and causally interpretable, even if the homeostatic condition fails to outperform its controls.
