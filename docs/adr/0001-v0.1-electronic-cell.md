# ADR 0001 — V0.1 Is the Electronic-Cell Stage

**Status:** Accepted for foundation review

## Context

Aweform's long-term goal is to investigate machine-native artificial life and adaptive cognition. The project could easily become over-designed by beginning with mature capabilities such as vision models, reinforcement learning, language, planning, or social behaviour.

The first credible developmental step is smaller: construct a bounded simulated organism whose continued viability depends on maintaining an internal energetic state through local sensing and action.

## Decision

V0.1 will implement only the minimum substrate necessary to study homeostasis and interoception.

The intended V0.1 substrate includes:

- finite internal energy;
- energetic cost of continued existence and/or action;
- environmental energy resources;
- limited local sensing;
- internal energy sensing (interoception);
- a small bounded action set;
- finite episodes;
- deterministic/reproducible experiment execution;
- transparent comparator controllers suitable for ablation.

The first implementation target is EXP-000: Interoception and Viability.

## Non-goals

V0.1 does not include:

- LLMs or human language;
- neural networks;
- reinforcement learning or PPO;
- JEPA or another learned world model;
- memory;
- curiosity or play;
- an awe mechanism;
- social behaviour;
- camera/image perception;
- ROS or physical robotics;
- networking;
- self-modification;
- reproduction;
- open-ended autonomy.

These are not rejected permanently. They are deferred until a later developmental problem justifies them.

## Reward decision

For EXP-000, the environment's reinforcement-learning reward should remain `0.0`.

Scientific evaluation should use direct viability and behavioural measurements rather than an optimisation reward. Changing this requires an explicit later decision.

## Design principle

The first controller should be deliberately transparent. Programmed homeostatic switching is an experimental condition, not a claim of emergent intelligence.

The purpose of V0.1 is to establish a clean substrate on which later developmental mechanisms can be tested, not to demonstrate sophisticated behaviour.

## Consequences

The initial codebase should remain small and easy to inspect. Dependencies and abstractions that exist only for anticipated later stages should not be added.

Success means the experiment is reproducible and causally interpretable, even if the homeostatic condition fails to outperform its controls.
