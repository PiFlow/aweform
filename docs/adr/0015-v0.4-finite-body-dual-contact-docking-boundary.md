# ADR 0015 — V0.4 Finite-Body Dual-Contact Docking Boundary

**Status:** Proposed while unmerged; **accepted only upon Flow-authorized
merge after the ADR-0013 independent-review gate passes on the exact current
HEAD**, without any further content change.

**Authorisation state:** This document authorizes no D-024 implementation while
unmerged. After the status condition is satisfied, this exact reviewed text
becomes the prospective D-024 finite-body docking and charging-contact
boundary. D-024 implementation still requires a fresh implementation
authorization from the new authoritative `main` SHA produced by acceptance of
this ADR.

**Scope:** A single durable V0.4 physical/contact boundary for D-024. This ADR
does not authorize D-024 source code, a behavioural run, a new learner, or any
change to the existing V0.4 energy/thermal model or organism-facing interface.

**References:** ADR 0012, ADR 0013, ADR 0014, and the evaluator-only
development record `development/D-017-shadow-rear-docking-pose-decomposition-
audit.md`.

## Context

D-017 evaluated a candidate finite body and rear two-contact morphology as a
post-hoc shadow over the unchanged D-014 controller and circular
body-centre-contact ecology. It found zero rear two-contact successes on its
declared three-seed support. The audit was evaluator-only: its shadow geometry
could not affect observations, action selection, transitions, charging,
energy, thermal state, RNG, or learning. D-017 did not authorize causal
morphology, reverse motion, docking control, additional sensing, or any other
organism boundary.

ADR 0012 opened the V0.4 physical energy and thermal boundary while allowing
the first slice to retain the existing point/body-centre movement and
charging-contact geometry. It requires a reviewed boundary when a geometry
change alters a durable physical or charging boundary. ADR 0014 supplements
that boundary for thermal operation and shutdown semantics. ADR 0013 governs
the independent exact-HEAD review required for this durable change.

D-020 through D-023 established descriptive fixed-action bookkeeping,
autonomous energy regulation, incidental-contact accounting, and repeated
cycle success under the existing circular charging semantics. Those results
remain valid. In particular, D-023's simple-controller success is not a reason
to harden the ecology arbitrarily; this ADR changes only the charging-contact
morphology needed for the explicitly bounded D-024 question.

## Decision

### A. Prospective D-024 morphology

After this ADR is accepted and a fresh D-024 implementation authorization is
issued, D-024 may replace circular body-centre charging contact with one
pose-attached finite-body rear dual-contact dock:

- Body length is `0.10` simulator units and body width is `0.08` simulator
  units.
- The body frame remains `+X` forward and `+Y` left.
- The rear face is `x = -0.05` in the body frame.
- The two corresponding body contacts are
  `(-0.05, +0.025)` and `(-0.05, -0.025)` in the body frame.
- The dock is fixed at orientation `phi = 0`.
- The corresponding dock contacts are fixed at the station centre offsets
  `(0.0, +0.025)` and `(0.0, -0.025)` in world coordinates.
- The two contact pairs retain their polarity/correspondence; a pair may not
  be swapped post hoc.

The finite body and its contacts define the causal docking morphology. The
body centre remains the kinematic state used by the existing simulator, and
the existing four-action point-centre kinematics remain unchanged. This is a
contact-geometry boundary, not authorization for rigid-body collision,
wheel dynamics, reverse motion, odometry, slip, or a detailed robot model.

### B. Exact valid docked initial pose

The exact valid docked initial pose for the first D-024 implementation is:

```text
station centre:       (0.50, 0.50)
dock orientation:     phi = 0
body centre:          (0.55, 0.50)
body heading:          0
```

At this pose, the body rear contacts are exactly:

```text
body rear + contact:  (0.50, 0.525)
body rear - contact:  (0.50, 0.475)
```

and the fixed dock contacts are the corresponding same two world points.
This is the exact valid two-contact setup for a station centre of `(0.50,
0.50)`; it replaces the historical point-body initial overlap for the
prospective D-024 docking setup only. The initial pose does not expose true
coordinates or dock geometry to the organism.

### C. Causal charging-contact predicate

The D-024 charging contact predicate is true exactly when both corresponding
rear body/dock contact distances satisfy the inclusive tolerance:

```text
distance(body_rear_plus, dock_plus) <= 0.01
and
distance(body_rear_minus, dock_minus) <= 0.01
```

The tolerance is `0.01` simulator units and applies independently to each
corresponding pair. One contact alone is insufficient. The predicate is
evaluated from the post-action pose using the existing transition causality;
it does not use future information, an evaluator success label, or a hidden
controller override. The existing D-020 charge acceptance, taper,
termination, and thermal accounting remain the charging consequences after
this predicate is true.

The predicate replaces the circular body-centre charging-contact condition for
D-024 only. It does not reinterpret any historical contact event or result.

### D. Preserved kinematics, physics, controller, and interface

The following remain unchanged for D-024:

- the existing four actions: `WAIT`, `TURN_LEFT`, `TURN_RIGHT`, and
  `MOVE_FORWARD`;
- point-centre movement kinematics, movement increment, turn semantics, and
  timestep;
- the accepted D-020 battery-energy bookkeeping, charging state machine,
  actuator/electronics accounting, signed ambient exchange, one-body thermal
  equation, and ADR-0014 thermal operating/shutdown thresholds;
- the D-021 controller boundary and its current fixed controller unless a
  later task explicitly authorizes a different developmental mechanism;
- exactly six organism-visible observation channels;
- reward exactly `0.0` on every transition and organism-facing `info == {}`;
- beacon values computed relative to the station centre;
- station-centre semantics for the beacon and any existing station-relative
  evaluator measurements.

The six channels remain the existing normalized own battery energy, beacon
left, beacon forward, beacon right, binary physical charging contact, and
normalized current own body temperature. The new dual-contact geometry does
not add a contact count, contact polarity, dock orientation, pose, distance,
heading, or threshold signal.

The station centre remains the beacon centre. The beacon does not move to a
rear contact, expose a dock-contact coordinate, or encode `phi`. The
organism-visible contact channel reports only the resulting binary dual-
contact predicate.

### E. Explicit exclusions

This ADR does not authorize:

- reverse action or reverse motion;
- odometry, wheel encoders, wheel proprioception, motor-current sensing, or
  other new sensor;
- wheel dynamics, differential-drive dynamics, slip, collision geometry,
  wall collision, wall heating, or wall avoidance;
- obstacles, complex physics, or a new thermal-stall model;
- a learner, predictor, learned world model, planning, counterfactual control,
  curiosity, play, reinforcement learning, PPO, deep RL, or reward shaping;
- any change to the existing action set, observation count, reward, info,
  beacon channels, beacon-centre semantics, or evaluator/organism information
  boundary;
- physical-device control, networking, external APIs, or hardware claims;
- any D-024 source implementation, experiment execution, result, artifact,
  seed reservation, or confirmatory evidence claim.

In particular, this ADR does not authorize changing the D-020/D-021/D-022/
D-023 source, development records, artifacts, results, or seed usage. It also
does not authorize retroactively treating D-017's shadow morphology as causal
or treating any D-020 through D-023 circular-charging result as a dual-contact
result.

## Historical and prospective scope

This is a new prospective D-024 boundary. It does not amend, reinterpret, or
invalidate D-017, D-020, D-021, D-022, or D-023. Their source, records,
artifacts, results, reward/info contracts, observation/action contracts,
physical semantics, and development-seed provenance remain exactly as
recorded under their own accepted executable SHAs.

The accepted V0.4 boundary in ADR 0012, the thermal amendment in ADR 0014,
and the V0.3 sensory/plasticity provenance boundary remain in force. This ADR
adds only the finite-body dual-contact docking morphology and its causal
charging-contact predicate for the future D-024 ecology.

## Implementation authorization after acceptance

Acceptance of this ADR does not itself authorize implementation in the same
branch or PR. After Flow-authorized merge, D-024 implementation requires a
separate, explicit authorization that names the new authoritative `main` SHA
and the implementation scope. The implementation must be based on that fresh
SHA. No D-024 source or behavioural experiment may be added under this ADR
PR.

## Consequences

- D-024 will have a minimal causal rear docking morphology grounded in the
  evaluator-only geometry already audited by D-017.
- Charging requires both corresponding rear contacts within `0.01`; incidental
  single-contact or body-centre overlap is not charging contact.
- The exact initial setup provides a valid dual-contact pose without adding
  organism-visible pose or dock information.
- The existing simple-controller and circular-charging results remain
  available as historical comparators; they are not silently erased or
  reclassified.
- A future D-024 result will test the consequences of this one geometry
  change, while keeping the declared energy, thermal, controller, observation,
  reward, info, and beacon-centre semantics comparable.
- The morphology may make contact reacquisition harder or may produce a null
  result. Either outcome is valid descriptive development evidence; this ADR
  does not predefine success or justify ecology hardening.

Flow alone authorizes merge. This ADR must not be merged until that gate is
satisfied and Flow authorizes it.
