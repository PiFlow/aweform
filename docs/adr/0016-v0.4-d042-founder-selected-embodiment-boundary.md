# ADR 0016 — D-042 Founder-Selected Fine-Turn and Front-Contact Boundary

**Status:** Proposed while unmerged; **accepted only upon Flow-authorized
merge after the ADR-0013 independent-review gate passes on the exact current
HEAD**, without any further content change.

**Authorisation state:** This document authorizes no D-042 source
implementation or behavioural run while unmerged. After the status condition
is satisfied, this exact reviewed text becomes the prospective D-042

fresh implementation authorization from the new authoritative `main` SHA
produced by acceptance of this ADR.

**Scope:** A single prospective V0.4 durable embodiment boundary for D-042

0015, which remains the historical authority for D-024 through D-041.

**References:** ADR 0012, ADR 0013, ADR 0015, and the evaluator-only
`development/D-041-front-beacon-sensory-sufficiency-audit.md`.

## Context

ADR 0012 opened the V0.4 physical energy and thermal boundary. ADR 0015
authorized the finite-body rear dual-contact morphology prospectively for
D-024 and later work performed under that boundary. D-024 through D-041,
including D-041's evaluator-only front-beacon audit, remain valid under their
historical contracts and results.

Flow has selected one future embodiment change for D-042: make the canonical
turn action 5 degrees and move the corresponding dual charging contacts from
the rear face to the front face. This is a founder-selected physical boundary
choice, not a capability that Aweform is expected to learn or discover. The
change requires a durable reviewed boundary before any source implementation.

The current source and historical records remain unchanged by this ADR PR.
In particular, the current `D020PhysicalConfig` turn value and D-024 rear
contact implementation continue to describe historical behaviour until a
separate, post-acceptance D-042 implementation authorization is issued.

## Decision

### A. Prospective canonical action and time semantics

After this ADR is accepted and a fresh D-042 implementation authorization is
issued, the canonical turn actions may use:

```text
TURN_LEFT  = +5 degrees = +pi/36 radians per action
TURN_RIGHT = -5 degrees = -pi/36 radians per action
```

The logical action set remains exactly:

```text
WAIT, TURN_LEFT, TURN_RIGHT, MOVE_FORWARD
```

Each turn remains one existing `dt = 0.1 s` action. The existing V0.4 turn
actuator electrical-power semantics remain exactly `0.65 W` per turn action;
the timestep and per-turn actuator power are not scaled down with the smaller
angle. Consequently, the angular speed is lower, and an equivalent total
angular displacement requires more actions, elapsed time, and turning energy
(for example, nine 5-degree actions for 45 degrees). Those are intentional
consequences of this selected embodiment.

`MOVE_FORWARD`, its existing magnitude, `WAIT`, and all other action timing
remain unchanged. This decision does not add a reverse action, a continuous
or variable-angle steering API, wheel controls, or wheel dynamics.

### B. Prospective front dual-contact morphology

The finite body remains length `0.10` and width `0.08` simulator units. Its
body frame remains `+X` forward and `+Y` left. For prospective D-042 causal
charging geometry, the corresponding body contacts move from the historical
rear face to the front face:

```text
front face:       x = +0.05
front-plus:       (+0.05, +0.025)
front-minus:      (+0.05, -0.025)
```

The dock remains fixed and station-centred at `phi = 0`. For station centre
`(0.50, 0.50)`, its corresponding contacts remain the station-relative
offsets:

```text
dock-plus:        (0.0, +0.025)
dock-minus:       (0.0, -0.025)
```

Polarity and plus/minus correspondence are preserved; body-plus must be
compared with dock-plus and body-minus with dock-minus. The finite body and
these contacts define the causal docking morphology, while the existing
point-centre kinematics remain in force.

### C. Exact prospective front-docked initial pose

The exact valid front-docked evaluator setup for the first D-042
implementation is:

```text
station centre:    (0.50, 0.50)
dock orientation:  phi = 0
body centre:       (0.45, 0.50)
body heading:      0
```

At this pose the two front body contacts coincide with the fixed dock
contacts:

```text
front-plus:        (0.50, 0.525)
front-minus:       (0.50, 0.475)
```

This is evaluator setup only. The organism receives no true coordinates,
heading, body geometry, dock geometry, pose, distance, or orientation.

### D. Causal charging-contact predicate

The prospective D-042 charging contact is true exactly when both
corresponding front body/dock pair errors independently satisfy the inclusive
tolerance:

```text
distance(body_front_plus, dock_plus) <= 0.01
and
distance(body_front_minus, dock_minus) <= 0.01
```

The tolerance is `0.01` simulator units. One pair alone is insufficient, and
the pairs may not be swapped post hoc. The predicate is evaluated from the
post-action pose under the existing causal transition order. Existing V0.4
charge acceptance, taper, termination, energy, and thermal consequences
remain unchanged after the predicate is true.

### E. Preserved boundaries

Unless separately authorized, D-042 preserves:

- the four logical actions, including the existing `MOVE_FORWARD` magnitude
  and `WAIT` semantics;
- `dt = 0.1 s` per transition and existing point-centre world-boundary clamp;
- the accepted D-020 battery, charger, energy, and thermal physics, including
  the existing actuator/electronics accounting and thresholds;
- exactly six organism-visible channels: normalized own battery energy,
  station-centred beacon left/forward/right, binary physical charging contact,
  and normalized current own body temperature;
- the existing station-centred L/F/R beacon semantics;
- reward exactly `0.0` on every transition and organism-facing `info == {}`;
- D-027 learner semantics and D-030 controller semantics;
- existing de-trap semantics and probabilities;
- existing RNG ownership, streams, and deterministic transition provenance;
- all evaluator-only versus organism-visible information separation.

The front-contact change does not add contact count, polarity, dock
orientation, pose, distance, heading, threshold, or any other observation
channel. The beacon remains centred on the station centre; it does not move
to a front contact or expose dock geometry.

### F. Explicit exclusions and historical preservation

This ADR does not authorize reverse motion, continuous or variable steering,
wheel dynamics, wheel control, odometry, encoders, motor-current sensing,
collision bodies, obstacles, complex physics, a camera, a new sensor or
beacon receptor, memory, planning, reinforcement learning, PPO, deep RL,
curiosity, play, a learned world model, an LLM, networking, external APIs,
physical-device control, self-modification, replication, or any other new
mechanism.

It does not authorize changing runtime/source semantics in this PR, running
D-042, modifying D-041, starting the sensorimotor-diversification audit, or
implementing Phase B. It does not authorize a new reward, reward shaping,
evaluator success label, hidden controller override, seed reservation,
confirmatory evidence claim, or scientific artifact.

ADR 0015 remains historical authority for D-024 through D-041. Their source,
records, artifacts, seed usage, action and observation contracts, rear-contact
semantics, D-041 evaluator-only receptor/readout, results, null results, and
interpretations are not amended, reclassified, or generalized by this ADR.
In particular, D-041 does not become evidence about the prospective D-042
front-contact embodiment, and its evaluator-only receptor remains outside the
organism's six-channel interface.

ADR 0012, ADR 0013, ADR 0014, ADR 0015, the V0.3 provenance boundary, and the
safety boundary remain in force except for the prospective D-042 choices
explicitly stated here. No historical result is a D-042 result.

## Implementation authorization after acceptance

Acceptance of this ADR does not authorize implementation in the same branch

explicit authorization naming the new authoritative `main` SHA and the

## Consequences

- A future D-042 implementation may test the founder-selected 5-degree
  canonical turn and front dual-contact morphology with a valid exact docked
  pose.
- The smaller turn angle changes angular speed and makes equivalent angular
  displacement cost more turns, time, and turn-actuator energy under the
  deliberately unchanged per-action timing and power.
- Charging remains a corresponding two-pair predicate with inclusive `0.01`
  tolerance; a single contact is not charging.
- Existing V0.4 physics, observations, reward/info, controller/learner
  semantics, de-trap, RNG, and station-centred beacon remain comparable.
- D-024 through D-041 remain historical records under ADR 0015; no prior
  source, artifact, result, or interpretation is silently rewritten.
